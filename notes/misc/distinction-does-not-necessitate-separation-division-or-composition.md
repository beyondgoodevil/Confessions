Distinction does not necessitate separation, division, or composition because something can have distinct aspects, roles, or relations while remaining unified and indivisible.

**Five Easy Examples:**

1. **The Sun** – The sun's light, heat, and mass are distinct, but they are not separate entities.
2. **A Human Mind** – Intellect and will are distinct faculties, but they belong to the same soul.
3. **A Fire** – The flame, warmth, and light are distinct properties, yet they remain one fire.
4. **A Single Word** – Its meaning, sound, and written form are distinct but not separate.
5. **A Coin** – The heads and tails sides are distinct but belong to the same coin.
