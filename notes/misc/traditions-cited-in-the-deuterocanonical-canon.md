---

### 1. 2 Maccabees 12:43-46 – Prayers for the Dead

- *"He also took up a collection, man by man, to the amount of two thousand drachmas of silver, and sent it to Jerusalem to provide for a sin offering... Therefore, he made atonement for the dead, so that they might be delivered from their sin."*
- **Tradition:** The idea of praying for the dead is not found in the Protestant canon but is a strong tradition upheld in Judaism and Christianity. This passage is often cited as a basis for practices like prayers for the souls of the departed.

### 2. Sirach (Ecclesiasticus) 44-50 – Praise of the Ancestors

- *"Let us now praise famous men, and our fathers in their generations..." (Sirach 44:1)*
- **Tradition:** Sirach honors the tradition of remembering the righteous ancestors of Israel, their wisdom, and their deeds, linking the idea of oral tradition and respect for heritage.

### 3. Baruch 3:9-4:4 – The Wisdom of the Ancients

- *"Hear the commandments of life, O Israel; give ear, and learn wisdom!" (Baruch 3:9)*
- **Tradition:** Baruch emphasizes the importance of following the wisdom handed down from past generations, including the commandments and teachings, reflecting a respect for tradition.

### 4. Wisdom of Solomon 18:24 – Priestly Vestments and Authority

- *"For on his long robe the whole world was depicted, and the glories of the ancestors were engraved on the four rows of stones, and your majesty on the diadem upon his head."*
- **Tradition:** This passage reflects the Jewish tradition of priestly authority and vestments as handed down through the line of Aaron, a key element in religious life.

### 5. Tobit 4:5-7 – Charitable Giving and Almsgiving

- *"Give alms from your possessions to all who live uprightly... Do not turn your face away from any poor person, and the face of God will not be turned away from you."*
- **Tradition:** Emphasizes the practice of almsgiving, a tradition in Jewish and Christian faith, particularly in Orthodox teaching on works of mercy and care for the poor.

### 6. 2 Maccabees 15:12-16 – Visions of the Dead and Intercession of Saints

- *"Onias, who had been high priest...was praying with outstretched hands for the whole body of the Jews...And he was seen by them as a man who had appeared as a guardian and protector..."*
- **Tradition:** This refers to the intercessory power of the righteous who have passed on, a tradition in Christianity regarding saints' intercession.

### 7. Judith 8:32-36 – Fasting and Prayer in Times of Crisis

- *"Then Judith said to them, 'Listen to me. I am about to do something that will go down through all generations of our descendants.'"*
- **Tradition:** The Jewish practice of fasting and communal prayer in times of crisis is highlighted here, with Judith offering herself in a way that sets an example for future generations.

### 8. Tobit 12:12 – Angels Carrying Prayers

- *"So now when you and Sarah prayed, it was I who brought the record of your prayer before the glory of the Lord."*
- **Tradition:** The idea of angels acting as intercessors and carrying prayers to God reflects a belief held in tradition, even though it is not clearly delineated in the Protestant canon.
