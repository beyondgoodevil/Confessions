12. **An Epistle of Paul to the Laodiceans**
    - *Colossians 4:16*

    "And when this epistle is read among you, cause that it be read also in the church of the Laodiceans..."  
    A letter written by Paul, now lost or possibly misidentified with existing ones.

13. **A Previous Letter to the Corinthians**
    - *1 Corinthians 5:9*

    "I wrote to you in my epistle not to keep company with sexually immoral people..."  
    Refers to a letter prior to First Corinthians, no longer extant.

14. **The Book of Enoch (1 Enoch)**
    - Explicitly quoted in *Jude 1:14–15*

    "Now Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied about these men also, saying..."  
    Not included in Protestant or Eastern Orthodox canon, though part of the Ethiopian “Orthodox” Church’s canon.

15. **The Assumption of Moses**
    - Alluded to in *Jude 1:9*, when Michael disputes with the devil about Moses' body.  
      Though not named directly, this incident is recorded in a known apocryphal text, now fragmentary.

16. **Book of Gad the Seer**
    - *1 Chronicles 29:29*  
      Cited alongside Nathan as a record of King David’s deeds.

17. **Laments for Josiah**
    - *2 Chronicles 35:25*  
      Jeremiah is said to have written laments for Josiah which were written in a now-lost book of laments.

18. **The Book of the Covenant**
    - *Exodus 24:7*  
      Possibly a now-lost legal or liturgical collection distinct from the Pentateuch.

19. **The Book of the Law of the Lord Given by Moses**
    - *2 Chronicles 34:14*  
      Could refer to Deuteronomy or a broader text, some believe it may have been a more original form.

20. **The Book of the Living**
    - *Psalm 69:28*

    "Let them be blotted out of the book of the living..."  
    Possibly symbolic, but some early fathers treated it as an actual heavenly record.
