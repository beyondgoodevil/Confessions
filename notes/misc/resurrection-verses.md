## 1. Romans 6:5

"For if we have been united with Him in a death like His, we shall certainly be united with Him in a resurrection like His."

- **Significance:** This verse establishes the strong connection between Christ's resurrection and the future resurrection of believers, highlighting the assurance of resurrection for those who are united with Him in faith.

## 2. 1 Corinthians 15:20-22

"But now Christ is risen from the dead, and has become the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep. For since by man came death, by Man also came the resurrection of the dead. For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ all shall be made alive."

- **Significance:** Paul describes Jesus as the "firstfruits" of the resurrection, indicating that His resurrection guarantees that all believers will also be resurrected.

## 3. 1 Corinthians 15:42-44

"So also is the resurrection of the dead. The body is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption. It is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power. It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body. There is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body."

- **Significance:** This passage contrasts the perishable nature of our earthly bodies with the glorious nature of our resurrected bodies, reinforcing the idea that believers will be raised in a manner similar to Christ.

## 4. Philippians 3:20-21

"For our citizenship is in heaven, from which we also eagerly wait for the Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, who will transform our lowly body that it may be conformed to His glorious body, according to the working by which He is able even to subdue all things to Himself."

- **Significance:** Paul assures believers that they will be transformed to be like Christ in His glorious resurrection body.

## 5. Colossians 3:4

"When Christ who is our life appears, then you also will appear with Him in glory."

- **Significance:** This verse affirms that believers will share in Christ's glory, highlighting the hope of a future resurrection that reflects His resurrection.

## 6. 1 Thessalonians 4:14

"For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, even so God will bring with Him those who sleep in Jesus."

- **Significance:** Paul reassures believers about the resurrection of those who have died in Christ, emphasizing that they will be raised just as Jesus was.

## 7. Revelation 20:6

"Blessed and holy is he who has part in the first resurrection. Over such the second death has no power, but they shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with Him a thousand years."

- **Significance:** This verse speaks to the blessing of participating in the resurrection, underscoring the hope of eternal life and reign with Christ.
