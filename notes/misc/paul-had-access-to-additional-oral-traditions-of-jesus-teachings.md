---

### 1. Acts 20:35:

Paul says:  
*"In all things I have shown you that by working hard in this way we must help the weak and remember the words of the Lord Jesus, how he himself said, 'It is more blessed to give than to receive.'"*

- **Not found** in any of the four Gospels. This saying of Jesus is unique to Paul's farewell speech in Acts.

### 2. 1 Thessalonians 4:15:

Paul writes:  
*"For this we declare to you by a word from the Lord, that we who are alive, who are left until the coming of the Lord, will not precede those who have fallen asleep."*

- **Not explicitly recorded** in the Gospels, although similar eschatological teachings of Jesus can be found in Matthew 24-25 and Mark 13, this specific phrasing from the "word of the Lord" is not mentioned in those accounts.

### 3. 1 Corinthians 7:10-11:

Paul says:  
*"To the married I give this charge (not I, but the Lord): the wife should not separate from her husband (but if she does, she should remain unmarried or else be reconciled to her husband), and the husband should not divorce his wife."*

- While **Jesus' teachings on marriage and divorce** are recorded in Matthew 19:6-9 and Mark 10:2-12, this particular phrasing of Paul seems to reflect a direct saying from Jesus not verbatim in the Gospels.

### 4. 1 Corinthians 9:14:

Paul writes:  
*"In the same way, the Lord commanded that those who proclaim the gospel should get their living by the gospel."*

- **No exact parallel** in the Gospels, although similar ideas about supporting ministry can be found in Matthew 10:10 and Luke 10:7. Paul, however, refers to this as a direct command of the Lord.

### 5. Romans 14:14:

Paul says:  
*"I know and am persuaded in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in itself, but it is unclean for anyone who thinks it unclean."*

- **No direct quote** of Jesus saying this is recorded in the Gospels, though there are similar teachings about food and purity in Mark 7:18-19. However, the specific phrasing here is unique to Paul.
