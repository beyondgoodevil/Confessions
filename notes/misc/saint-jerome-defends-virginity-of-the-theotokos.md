## 1. Defense of Mary as Theotokos

- Jerome strongly affirmed the title of **Theotokos**, emphasizing that Mary bore not only the human Jesus but also the divine Word (Logos). He argued that recognizing Mary as **Theotokos** was essential for maintaining the doctrine of the Incarnation, as it highlights the unity of Christ's divine and human natures.

## 2. Opposition to Nestorianism

- In response to the teachings of Nestorius, who argued against calling Mary **Theotokos** and proposed the separation of Christ's two natures, Jerome defended the orthodox position. He asserted that denying this title undermined the belief in the true nature of Christ as both fully God and fully man.

## 3. Promotion of Mary's Role in Salvation History

- Jerome emphasized Mary's unique role in salvation history, portraying her not only as the mother of Christ but also as a model of faith and obedience. He saw her acceptance of God's will as a critical moment in the story of salvation.

## 4. Veneration of Mary

- While Jerome advocated for honoring Mary, he stressed that the veneration she received should lead believers closer to Christ. He viewed the honor given to Mary as a reflection of her unique position in the economy of salvation.

## Conclusion

Through his writings, Saint Jerome played a vital role in affirming Mary's title as **Theotokos**, opposing heretical views that sought to diminish her significance. His defense highlighted her essential role in the mystery of the Incarnation and the broader narrative of salvation, contributing to the theological understanding of Mary in the early Church.
