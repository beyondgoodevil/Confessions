Jesus promised to send the Holy Spirit to guide the Church in several passages in the New Testament. Here are the key references:

### 1. John 14:16-17

- Jesus promises that the Father will give another Advocate, the Spirit of truth, to be with the disciples forever. The world cannot accept Him, but they know Him, for He lives with them and will be in them. *(Paraphrased; the image quotes a modern copyrighted translation.)*

### 2. John 14:26

- Here, Jesus continues: the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in His name, will teach them all things and remind them of everything He has said. *(Paraphrased.)*

### 3. John 15:26

- Jesus says that when the Advocate comes, whom He will send from the Father, the Spirit of truth who goes out from the Father, He will testify about Him. *(Paraphrased.)*

### 4. John 16:13

- Jesus explains further: when the Spirit of truth comes, He will guide them into all the truth. He will not speak on His own but only what He hears, and will tell them what is yet to come. *(Paraphrased.)*

### 5. Acts 1:4-5

- After His resurrection, Jesus instructs His disciples not to leave Jerusalem but to wait for the gift the Father promised: John baptized with water, but in a few days they will be baptized with the Holy Spirit. *(Paraphrased.)*

### 6. Acts 2:1-4

- This passage describes the fulfillment of Jesus' promise on the Day of Pentecost when the Holy Spirit descended upon the apostles, empowering them to spread the Gospel.

These passages collectively demonstrate Jesus' promise to send the Holy Spirit to guide, teach, and empower the Church in its mission.
