1. **Sacramental Theology in the Orthodox Church:**
    - The Orthodox Church teaches that the sacraments, including the Eucharist, are only efficacious within the Church, which possesses the fullness of truth and grace.
    - This idea is based on the understanding that the Church is the Body of Christ, and the sacraments are life-giving only when administered within that body.
2. **General Teaching on Schism and Sacraments:**
    - While specific wording like "dead meal" may not be found in St. Athanasius, the **early Church Fathers**, including **St. Cyprian of Carthage**, expressed similar ideas. St. Cyprian, for instance, famously taught:  
      *"There is no salvation outside the Church" (Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus).* He emphasized that sacraments performed outside the Church lose their life-giving power.
3. **Writings of St. Athanasius:**
    - In his defense of the Nicene faith against the **Arians**, St. Athanasius emphasized the importance of true doctrine and unity with the Church. He argued that separation from the Church leads to separation from the grace of Christ, which would include the sacraments like the Eucharist.
4. **Canonical Decisions and Patristic Consensus:**
    - This theological stance was later reflected in **canonical texts** and teachings, emphasizing that sacraments outside the Church lack efficacy. Though Athanasius may not have used the exact phrase, his commitment to Orthodox ecclesiology and sacramental theology would affirm this position.
