One thread runs from Genesis to Revelation: **God wants to dwell with His people**. Each stage is a step closer: a garden, a tent, a house, a body, a people, a city. Verses KJV.

## 1. Eden: The First Sanctuary

- Gen 2:8: "And the LORD God **planted a garden eastward in Eden**; and there he put the man whom he had formed."
- Gen 3:8: "they heard the voice of the LORD God **walking in the garden** in the cool of the day." The same verb (*hithallek*) is used of God walking among Israel in the tabernacle (Lev 26:12).
- Gen 2:15: Adam put there "**to dress it and to keep it**". The same two verbs (*abad*, *shamar*) are used of the Levites' service in the tabernacle (Num 3:7-8).
- Gen 3:24: after the Fall, "**cherubims**... to keep the way of the tree of life", at the **east**. Cherubim guard the temple's holy of holies too, and its entrance faced east.
- Ezek 28:13-14: "Thou hast been in **Eden the garden of God**... upon **the holy mountain of God**." Eden as a mountain sanctuary.

## 2. The Tabernacle

- Exod 25:8-9: "And let them make me a **sanctuary**; that **I may dwell among them**. According to all that I shew thee, after **the pattern** of the tabernacle."
- Exod 29:45: "And **I will dwell among the children of Israel**, and will be their God."
- Exod 40:34-35: "Then **a cloud covered the tent** of the congregation, and **the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle**. And Moses was not able to enter."
- Lev 26:11-12: "I will set my tabernacle among you... And **I will walk among you, and will be your God, and ye shall be my people**."
- Heb 8:5: the priests "serve unto **the example and shadow of heavenly things**, as Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount." The tabernacle copies a heavenly original.

## 3. Solomon's Temple

- 2 Sam 7:5, 12-13: David wants to build God a house; God answers that David's seed "**shall build an house for my name**, and I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever." The house and the king are bound together. See [[Kingship of Christ]].
- 1 Kgs 8:10-11: at the dedication "**the cloud filled the house of the LORD**... for **the glory of the LORD had filled the house**."
- 1 Kgs 8:27: Solomon: "But will God indeed dwell on the earth? behold, **the heaven and heaven of heavens cannot contain thee**; how much less this house that I have builded?"
- Ps 27:4: "One thing have I desired of the LORD... that I may **dwell in the house of the LORD** all the days of my life."

## 4. The Glory Leaves, and a Greater Temple Is Promised

- Ezek 10:18: "Then **the glory of the LORD departed** from off the threshold of the house." Before the Babylonian destruction.
- Ezek 43:2-5: the glory **returns** to a new temple, "from the way of the east... and **the glory of the LORD filled the house**."
- Ezek 47:1: "**waters issued out from under the threshold** of the house eastward." A river of life from the temple, like the river from Eden (Gen 2:10).
- Hag 2:7, 9: of the rebuilt temple: "**the desire of all nations shall come**: and I will fill this house with glory... **The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former**."
- Mal 3:1: "**the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple**."

## 5. Christ's Body Is the Temple

- John 1:14: "And the Word was made flesh, and **dwelt among us**" (Greek *eskēnōsen*, "**tabernacled**"), "and we beheld **his glory**." The glory that filled the tabernacle is now in a human body.
- John 2:19-21: "**Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up**... But he spake of **the temple of his body**."
- Matt 12:6: "in this place is **one greater than the temple**."
- Col 2:9: "in him dwelleth **all the fulness of the Godhead bodily**."
- Matt 27:51: "**the veil of the temple was rent in twain** from the top to the bottom." The way into the holy of holies opened.
- Heb 10:19-20: "boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus, by a new and living way... **through the veil, that is to say, his flesh**."
- Heb 9:11: Christ "an high priest... by **a greater and more perfect tabernacle, not made with hands**."
- John 4:21, 23: "the hour cometh, when ye shall **neither in this mountain, nor yet at Jerusalem**, worship the Father... the true worshippers shall worship the Father **in spirit and in truth**."

## 6. The Church Is the Temple

- 1 Cor 3:16-17: "Know ye not that **ye are the temple of God**, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?" ("ye" plural: the community.)
- Eph 2:19-22: "built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone; In whom all the building fitly framed together **groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord**: In whom ye also are builded together **for an habitation of God through the Spirit**."
- 1 Pet 2:5: "Ye also, as **lively stones**, are built up **a spiritual house**, an holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices."
- 2 Cor 6:16: "for **ye are the temple of the living God**; as God hath said, **I will dwell in them, and walk in them**" (quoting Lev 26:12).
- Acts 2:3: at Pentecost the fire rests on the disciples as the glory-cloud rested on the tabernacle. See [[Jeremiah, Acts 2: The Beginning of the Church, Where God Dwells With His People]].

## 7. The Believer Is the Temple

- 1 Cor 6:19-20: "know ye not that **your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost** which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore **glorify God in your body**."
- John 14:23: "If a man love me... my Father will love him, and **we will come unto him, and make our abode with him**."
- Rom 12:1: "present your bodies **a living sacrifice**, holy, acceptable unto God."

## 8. The New Jerusalem: No Temple

- Rev 21:2-3: "the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven... **Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them**, and they shall be his people."
- Rev 21:16: the city is a perfect **cube**, like the holy of holies (1 Kgs 6:20). The whole city is the holy of holies.
- Rev 21:22: "And **I saw no temple therein: for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are the temple of it**."
- Rev 22:1-2: "**a pure river of water of life**, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb... **the tree of life**." Eden restored, and Ezekiel's temple river.
- Rev 22:4: "they shall **see his face**." What Adam lost and Moses couldn't have (Exod 33:20).

## 9. Summary

| Stage | Where God dwells | Key verse |
|---|---|---|
| Eden | the garden on the mountain | Gen 3:8 |
| Tabernacle | a tent, a copy of heaven | Exod 25:8 |
| Temple | Solomon's house | 1 Kgs 8:11 |
| Exile | the glory departs | Ezek 10:18 |
| Incarnation | Christ's body | John 2:21 |
| Pentecost | the Church | Eph 2:21-22 |
| Each Christian | the body | 1 Cor 6:19 |
| The End | the whole city; God and the Lamb | Rev 21:22 |

*Mine:* the movement is outward. In Eden God is with two people; at the end the whole creation is the holy of holies. The Orthodox church building copies this (narthex, nave, altar as the holy of holies, iconostasis as the veil now opened at the Royal Doors).
