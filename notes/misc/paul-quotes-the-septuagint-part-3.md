### 10. 1 Corinthians 15:54

> **“Death is swallowed up in victory.”**

From **Isaiah 25:8 (LXX)**.

- **LXX:** “Death is swallowed up in victory.”
- **MT:** “He will swallow up death forever.”

Though similar, only the **LXX** has the exact Greek words that Paul quotes.

### Summary

St. Paul overwhelmingly quotes from the **Septuagint**, not the **Masoretic Text**. In many instances:

- The **wording differs** significantly
- The **theology is altered**
- And only the **LXX** supports his **Christological interpretations**

This reinforces the Orthodox position that the **Septuagint is the true Old Testament**, providentially preserved for the Church and used by Christ, the Apostles, and the Fathers.
