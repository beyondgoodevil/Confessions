## Ministry of Apollos

- **18:24** – Apollos, a Jew born at Alexandria, eloquent and mighty in the Scriptures, comes to Ephesus.
- **18:25** – He has been instructed in the way of the Lord and, fervent in spirit, teaches accurately the things of the Lord, though he knows only the baptism of John.
- **18:26** – He speaks boldly in the synagogue. Aquila and Priscilla hear him, take him aside, and explain the way of God to him more accurately.
- **18:27** – When he wishes to cross to Achaia, the brethren write urging the disciples to receive him; on arriving he greatly helps those who had believed through grace.
- **18:28** – He vigorously refutes the Jews in public, showing from the Scriptures that Jesus is the Christ.
