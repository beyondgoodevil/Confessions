---
title: God the Father created through the Son and with the Holy Spirit
tags: [theology, trinity, scripture]
summary: Creation as the work of the Trinity — from the Father, through the Son, with the Holy Spirit.
---

### From the Father:

- *"For from Him and through Him and to Him are all things. To Him be glory forever. Amen."* — Romans 11:36
- The Father is the **source and cause** of creation, from whom all things originate. Creation begins **with His divine will**, as He brings all things into existence out of nothing (*ex nihilo*).

### Through the Son:

- *"All things were made through Him, and without Him nothing was made that was made."* — John 1:3
- *"For by Him all things were created that are in heaven and that are on earth, visible and invisible."* — Colossians 1:16
- The Son is the **divine Logos (Word)** through whom all things are made. He is the Wisdom of God, executing the Father’s will in creation.

### With the Holy Spirit:

- *"By the word of the Lord the heavens were established, and all the host of them by the breath of His mouth."* — Psalm 32(33):6 (LXX)
- The Holy Spirit is the **Giver of Life**, as affirmed in the Nicene Creed. He perfects, sanctifies, and brings creation to fulfillment.

### Thus, creation is the work of the Holy Trinity:

- **The Father** is the **source and cause** of creation, from whom all things originate.
- **The Son** is the **divine Logos (Word)** through whom all things are made.
- **The Holy Spirit perfects, gives life, and brings creation to fulfillment**.
