The image is a screenshot of Judges 13:2-22 in the New King James Version, with each occurrence of "God" highlighted. The NKJV text is copyrighted, so this note outlines the passage verse by verse.

- **13:2** – Manoah, a Danite from Zorah, has a wife who is barren.
- **13:3-5** – The Angel of the Lord appears to her: she will bear a son. She must avoid wine and anything unclean; no razor is to touch his head, for he will be a Nazirite to ==God== from the womb and will begin to deliver Israel from the Philistines.
- **13:6-7** – She tells her husband that a Man of ==God== came, with a countenance like the Angel of ==God==, very awesome; she did not ask where He was from, and He did not give His name.
- **13:8** – Manoah prays that the Man of ==God== be sent again to teach them what to do for the child.
- **13:9-10** – ==God== listens; the Angel of ==God== comes again to the woman in the field, and she runs to fetch Manoah.
- **13:11-14** – Manoah asks if He is the Man who spoke to her; He answers, "I am," and repeats the instructions.
- **13:15-16** – Manoah offers to prepare a young goat. The Angel will not eat, but says a burnt offering must be offered to the Lord. Manoah does not yet know He is the Angel of the Lord.
- **13:17-18** – Manoah asks His name; He replies, why ask, seeing it is wonderful?
- **13:19-21** – Manoah offers the goat on the rock; the Angel of the Lord ascends in the flame of the altar. They fall on their faces, and Manoah knows who He was.
- **13:22** – Manoah says to his wife that they will surely die, because they have seen ==God==.
