### From Suffering to Glory

- **8:18** – Present sufferings are not worth comparing with the glory to be revealed in us.
- **8:19** – Creation eagerly waits for the revealing of the sons of God.
- **8:20** – Creation was subjected to futility, not willingly, but by Him who subjected it in hope.
- **8:21** (highlighted) – ==Creation itself will be delivered from the bondage of corruption== into the glorious liberty of the children of God.
- **8:22** – The whole creation groans and labors with birth pangs until now.
- **8:23** – We too, who have the firstfruits of the Spirit, groan inwardly, waiting for the adoption, the redemption of our body.
- **8:24-25** – We were saved in this hope; hope that is seen is not hope, so we wait for what we do not see with perseverance.
