1. **Total Depravity** is a key tenet of Calvinism, part of the TULIP acronym (which stands for Total Depravity, Unconditional Election, Limited Atonement, Irresistible Grace, and Perseverance of the Saints). **Total Depravity** means that due to the Fall, every part of human nature is corrupted by sin, making humans incapable of choosing God or doing good on their own. Calvinism emphasizes the profound sinful nature of humanity without divine grace.
2. **Infallibility**, on the other hand, suggests the inability to make mistakes, which is incompatible with the doctrine of Total Depravity. According to Calvinist theology, humans, in their fallen state, are prone to error, both morally and intellectually, without the intervention of God's grace.

Therefore, Calvinism does not claim that humans, in their fallen state, are infallible; instead, it underscores human fallibility due to sin. The phrase as written appears contradictory in Calvinist terms.
