### Swami Vivekananda and John D. Rockefeller Sr.

Swami Vivekananda is known to have met John D. Rockefeller Sr. in the 1890s, during his stay in the United States. This meeting is often recounted in stories about Vivekananda's influence on Rockefeller's philanthropy. According to these accounts:

1. Rockefeller was initially uninterested in meeting Vivekananda, but after some persuasion, he did.
2. Vivekananda reportedly told Rockefeller that his immense wealth was given to him by God to serve humanity and that he had a duty to use it for the greater good.
3. This conversation is sometimes credited with inspiring Rockefeller to establish his philanthropic foundations, including the Rockefeller Foundation (1913).

### Was Vivekananda Involved in Ecumenism?

- While Vivekananda was a Hindu monk, he advocated for religious unity and interfaith dialogue.
- His speech at the 1893 Parliament of the World's Religions in Chicago is one of the most famous moments in interfaith history, promoting the idea that all religions lead to the same truth.
- However, he was not directly involved in Christian ecumenism (the movement to unite different Christian denominations).
- His influence was more on interfaith dialogue rather than Christian unity.
