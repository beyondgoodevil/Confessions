The image is a screenshot of Genesis 32:24-30 in the New King James Version, with "God" highlighted in verses 28 and 30. The NKJV text is copyrighted, so this note outlines the passage verse by verse.

- **32:24** – Jacob is left alone, and a Man wrestles with him until the breaking of day.
- **32:25** – Seeing He does not prevail, the Man touches the socket of Jacob's hip and puts it out of joint.
- **32:26** – He says, "Let Me go, for the day breaks." Jacob answers that he will not let Him go unless He blesses him.
- **32:27-28** – Asked his name, Jacob gives it. He is told his name will now be Israel, for he has struggled with ==God== and with men and has prevailed.
- **32:29** – Jacob asks His name; He replies, why do you ask?, and blesses him there.
- **32:30** – Jacob names the place Peniel: "For I have seen ==God== face to face, and my life is preserved."
