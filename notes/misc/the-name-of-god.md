In Scripture a name isn't a label. It stands for the person: their presence, character and authority. So God's **Name** is almost a way of saying God Himself as He comes to us. The theme runs from the burning bush to "the name which is above every name". Verses KJV.

## 1. "I AM"

- Exod 3:13-14: "when I come unto the children of Israel... and they shall say to me, What is his name? what shall I say unto them? And God said unto Moses, **I AM THAT I AM**: and he said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, **I AM hath sent me unto you**."
- Exod 3:15: "**The LORD God of your fathers**... **this is my name for ever**, and this is my memorial unto all generations."
- The four letters YHWH (the *Tetragrammaton*) come from the verb "to be". Jews stopped saying it aloud and read *Adonai*, "Lord"; the Septuagint has *Kyrios*. KJV prints it "LORD" in capitals.
- The Septuagint renders "I AM" as ***ho ōn***, "**the One who is**". Those letters are on Christ's halo in every Orthodox icon. See [[Bible: Exodus 3, I AM WHO I AM, Theophany]].
- Exod 6:3: "by my name JEHOVAH was I not known to them." Abraham knew God, but not by this name.

## 2. The Name Is God's Character

- Exod 33:19: "I will make all my goodness pass before thee, and **I will proclaim the name of the LORD** before thee."
- Exod 34:5-7: "the LORD descended in the cloud... and **proclaimed the name of the LORD**. The LORD, The LORD God, **merciful and gracious, longsuffering, and abundant in goodness and truth**." When God declares His name, He declares what He's like.
- Exod 20:7: "Thou shalt not **take the name of the LORD thy God in vain**." Using God's name falsely or lightly is treating God Himself that way.
- Lev 24:16: "he that **blasphemeth the name of the LORD**, he shall surely be put to death."

## 3. The Name Dwells in the Temple

- Deut 12:11: "a place which the LORD your God shall choose **to cause his name to dwell there**."
- 1 Kgs 8:29: "that thine eyes may be open toward this house... even toward the place of which thou hast said, **My name shall be there**."
- 1 Kgs 9:3: "I have hallowed this house... **to put my name there for ever**; and mine eyes and mine heart shall be there perpetually."
- God can't be contained, yet His Name is present. An early version of the distinction between God in Himself and God as He makes Himself present. See [[The Temple and Its Fulfilment]], [[Essence-Energies Distinction]].

## 4. The Name in the Psalms

- Ps 8:1: "O LORD our Lord, **how excellent is thy name in all the earth**!"
- Ps 20:1, 7: "the LORD hear thee in the day of trouble; **the name of the God of Jacob defend thee**... Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but **we will remember the name of the LORD our God**."
- Ps 54:1: "**Save me, O God, by thy name**."
- Ps 113:2-3: "**Blessed be the name of the LORD** from this time forth and for evermore. From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same the LORD's name is to be praised." (Sung at Orthodox Vespers.)
- Ps 124:8: "**Our help is in the name of the LORD**, who made heaven and earth." Said by the bishop at the end of the Liturgy.
- Prov 18:10: "**The name of the LORD is a strong tower**: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe."
- Joel 2:32: "whosoever shall **call on the name of the LORD** shall be delivered." Quoted by Peter (Acts 2:21) and Paul (Rom 10:13) of **Jesus**.

## 5. The Angel Who Bears the Name

- Exod 23:20-21: "I send **an Angel** before thee... Beware of him, and obey his voice, provoke him not; for he will not pardon your transgressions: for **my name is in him**." An angel who can forgive sins and bears God's name. The Fathers read this as the pre-incarnate Son.
- Judg 13:18: the angel to Manoah: "Why askest thou thus after my name, seeing it is **secret**?" (Hebrew *peli*, "wonderful", the same word as Isa 9:6.)
- Isa 9:6: "**his name shall be called Wonderful**, Counsellor, The mighty God."

## 6. Jesus Says "I AM"

- **Jesus** (*Yeshua*) means "YHWH saves". Matt 1:21: "thou shalt call his name JESUS: for **he shall save his people from their sins**."
- Matt 1:23: "they shall call his name **Emmanuel**, which being interpreted is, **God with us**."
- John 8:58: "Before Abraham was, **I am**." Then "took they up stones to cast at him": they understood the claim.
- John 18:5-6: "Jesus saith unto them, **I am he**... As soon then as he had said unto them, I am he, **they went backward, and fell to the ground**."
- Mark 6:50 (walking on the water): "Be of good cheer: **it is I**" (*egō eimi*). Cf. Job 9:8: God alone "treadeth upon the waves of the sea". See [[Jesus Walking on Water: Eternal Act, Essence-Energy Distinction]].
- The seven "I am" sayings in John with predicates: bread of life (6:35), light of the world (8:12), the door (10:9), good shepherd (10:11), resurrection and life (11:25), way, truth and life (14:6), true vine (15:1).
- John 17:6, 11: "**I have manifested thy name** unto the men which thou gavest me... Holy Father, keep through **thine own name** those whom thou hast given me." See [[Bible: John 17, Jesus Has the Father's Glory]].

## 7. The Name Above Every Name

- Phil 2:9-11: "Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and **given him a name which is above every name**: That at the name of Jesus **every knee should bow**... And that **every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord**."
    - This quotes Isa 45:23, where **YHWH** says: "unto me every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear." Paul applies God's own words to Jesus. The name given is "Lord", *Kyrios*, the Septuagint's word for YHWH.
- Heb 1:4: "**he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name** than they" (the angels).
- Acts 4:12: "**there is none other name under heaven** given among men, whereby we must be saved."
- Rev 19:12-13: "he had **a name written, that no man knew, but he himself**... and **his name is called The Word of God**."

## 8. In the Name

- **Baptism**: Matt 28:19, "baptizing them **in the name** of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost." One name, three Persons. See [[The Trinity]].
- **Prayer**: John 14:13-14, "**whatsoever ye shall ask in my name**, that will I do." John 16:24, "hitherto have ye asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive."
- **Healing**: Acts 3:6, "**In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth** rise up and walk." Acts 3:16: "**his name through faith in his name hath made this man strong**."
- **Exorcism**: Mark 16:17, "**In my name shall they cast out devils**." Acts 19:13-16: the sons of Sceva use the name without faith, and the demon overpowers them. Not magic.
- **Gathering**: Matt 18:20, "where two or three are gathered together **in my name**, there am I in the midst of them."
- **Everything**: Col 3:17, "whatsoever ye do in word or deed, **do all in the name of the Lord Jesus**."
- **Suffering**: Acts 5:41, "rejoicing that they were counted worthy to **suffer shame for his name**."

## 9. The Jesus Prayer

*Mine.*

- The whole theme ends up in Orthodox practice in the **Jesus Prayer**: "Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner." Calling on the Name continually (1 Thess 5:17, "pray without ceasing"). See [[The Jesus Prayer and Hesychasm]].
- The Fathers: the Name isn't magic, but it's not just a word either. It makes Christ present to the one who calls with faith. (In 1913 there was a controversy on Mt Athos, the *imiaslavie* or "name-glorifiers", over whether the Name *is* God. The Russian Church condemned the strong version; the question isn't fully settled.)
- Lord's Prayer: "**Hallowed be thy name**" comes first. Ezek 36:23: "I will sanctify my great name." God's name is made holy when His people are.
