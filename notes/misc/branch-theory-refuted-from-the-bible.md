## 1. Jesus Is with Sinners but Commands Them to Stop Sinning

### The Woman Caught in Adultery

*"Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more."* — John 8:11 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** Jesus does not reject the sinner but calls her to repentance. He is **with** her, but He does not allow her to remain in sin.

### The Healing of the Paralytic at the Pool

*"Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee."* — John 5:14 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** Jesus heals the man but warns that sin leads to worse consequences. He shows mercy **while demanding repentance**.

### Jesus Eating with Sinners, Calling Them to Repentance

*"I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance."* — Luke 5:32 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** Jesus is with sinners not to affirm them in their ways, but to **bring them into holiness** and **into His Church**.

## 2. People Who Do Mighty Works in Christ’s Name but Are Not Truly Part of His Church

### False Disciples Who Claim to Act in Jesus’ Name

*"Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven. Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity."* — Matthew 7:21-23 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** Some people act **in the name of Christ** but are **not truly part of the Church**. Jesus rejects those who claim His power yet do **not live in obedience** to His will.

### The Example of the Sons of Sceva (Unauthorized Use of Christ’s Name)

*"And the evil spirit answered and said, Jesus I know, and Paul I know; but who are ye? And the man in whom the evil spirit was leaped on them, and overcame them, and prevailed against them, so that they fled out of that house naked and wounded."* — Acts 19:15-16 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** The name of Jesus is **not a magical formula**—only those truly **within His Church** have authority over demons.

## 3. Those Who Are Not Against Us Are with Us (But They Must Be Part of the Church, Not Independent)

### A Man Casting Out Demons in Jesus’ Name

*"And John answered him, saying, Master, we saw one casting out devils in thy name, and he followeth not us: and we forbad him, because he followeth not us. But Jesus said, Forbid him not: for there is no man which shall do a miracle in my name, that can lightly speak evil of me. For he that is not against us is on our part."* — Mark 9:38-40 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** Those who act **in Christ’s name with faith** are doing the work of God, but they must eventually be **part of the Church, not isolated from it**.

## Eastern Orthodox Interpretation and Unity in the Church

### The Church as the Only True Place of Authority

*"That thou mayest know how thou oughtest to behave thyself in the house of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth."* — 1 Timothy 3:15 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** The true **authority and power of Christ** reside in **His Church**, not in individuals acting independently.

### One Body, One Spirit

*"There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling; One Lord, one faith, one baptism."* — Ephesians 4:4-5 (KJV)

- **Meaning:** There is **only one Church**, and all who act in Christ’s name must be **united in it**.
