The **Twelve Tribes of Israel** descend from Jacob's sons and grandsons:

1. **Reuben** – Jacob's firstborn with Leah, settled east of the Jordan but lost leadership due to his sin (Genesis 49:4).
2. **Simeon** – Second son of Leah, absorbed into Judah's territory (Joshua 19:1-9).
3. **Levi** – Tribe of priests with no land inheritance, served in the Tabernacle and Temple (Numbers 18:20-24).
4. **Judah** – Fourth son, leading tribe, from which King David and Christ (Revelation 5:5) descended.
5. **Dan** – Son of Bilhah, settled in northern Israel (Judges 18).
6. **Naphtali** – Also Bilhah's, inhabited the region around the Sea of Galilee (Joshua 19:32-39).
7. **Gad** – Son of Zilpah, settled east of the Jordan (Numbers 32:1-5).
8. **Asher** – Zilpah's second son, known for fertile lands in the north (Deuteronomy 33:24).
9. **Issachar** – Leah's son, occupied a region in central Israel (Joshua 19:17-23).
10. **Zebulun** – Leah's, located near the Mediterranean coast (Joshua 19:10-16).
11. **Joseph** – Split into two tribes: Ephraim and Manasseh (Jacob's grandsons), each receiving its own land (Genesis 48).
12. **Benjamin** – Youngest, tribe loyal to Judah, with Jerusalem in its territory (Joshua 18:11-28).
