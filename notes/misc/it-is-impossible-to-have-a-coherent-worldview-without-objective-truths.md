title: It Is Impossible to Have a Coherent Worldview Without Objective Truths
tags: [philosophy, apologetics, logic]
summary: What collapses without objective truth, with ten examples.
---

A coherent worldview requires internal consistency. But consistency depends on objective logical laws, like the Law of Non-Contradiction. If such laws are subjective, then contradictions become acceptable, and reason itself breaks down.

The claim *"There is no objective truth"* refutes itself. If true, it is an objective truth, which contradicts the claim. If false, it need not be believed. Either way, it collapses into incoherence.

**Without objective truths:**

- Knowledge becomes personal opinion.
- Morality becomes arbitrary preference.
- Justice turns into power dynamics.
- Language loses meaning.
- Science becomes narrative, not discovery.

**For example:**

1. Torture and kindness are morally equal if no objective good exists.
2. “2 + 2 = 4” becomes a cultural opinion.
3. A murderer’s claim of innocence is as valid as the victim’s suffering.
4. Courts have no reason to uphold rights—they're just conventions.
5. Teaching becomes indoctrination; truth has no standard.
6. History becomes mythology with no “what really happened.”
7. Rights are illusions; whoever holds power defines them.
8. Logic becomes optional—no reason to prefer valid over invalid arguments.
9. Love loses meaning; it’s just a chemical impulse.
10. Theology becomes poetry—nothing to believe, only to feel.

From the Orthodox Christian view, objective truth is not an idea but a Person—Christ, Who says, *“I am the Truth”* (John 14:6). The world is intelligible because it is created through the Logos, and the logoi within all things reflect divine order. Denying objective truth is not just error—it is rebellion against the very structure of being.

Thus, without objective truths, there is no coherence, no meaning, no salvation—only chaos.
