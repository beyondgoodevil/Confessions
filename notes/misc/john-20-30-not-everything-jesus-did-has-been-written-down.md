## Seeing and Believing

- **20:24-25** – Thomas was absent when Jesus came. Told that the others have seen the Lord, he says he will not believe unless he sees and touches the marks of the nails and the wound in His side.
- **20:26-27** – Eight days later, the doors being shut, Jesus stands among them, says "Peace to you," and invites Thomas to touch His hands and side and to believe.
- **20:28** – Thomas answers, "My Lord and my God!"
- **20:29** – Jesus says Thomas has believed because he has seen; blessed are those who have not seen and yet believed.

## That You May Believe

- **20:30** (highlighted) – Jesus did many other signs before His disciples ==which are not written in this book==.
- **20:31** – These are written so that the reader may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and have life in His name.
