## Types of the Church in the Old Testament

- **Eve** (Genesis 2:21-23)  
  Eve, created from Adam's side, prefigures the Church, born from the side of Christ (the second Adam) when He was pierced on the cross (John 19:34).
- **Noah's Ark** (Genesis 6-9, 1 Peter 3:20-21)  
  The Ark, which saves Noah's family from the flood, represents the Church, the vessel of salvation for humanity.
- **The Temple** (1 Kings 6-8)  
  The physical Temple, where God's presence dwelled among the Israelites, prefigures the Church, the spiritual temple where God dwells among His people.
- **The Twelve Tribes of Israel** (Genesis 49)  
  The twelve tribes of Israel, God's chosen people, prefigure the Church, which is composed of all nations united in Christ.
- **Zion/Jerusalem** (Isaiah 2:2-4, Hebrews 12:22)  
  Zion, the holy city, is a type of the Church, the new Jerusalem, where God gathers His people from all nations.
- **The Vine** (Psalm 80:8-16, Isaiah 5:1-7, John 15:1-8)  
  Israel is described as a vine planted by God, prefiguring the Church, which is Christ's vineyard, and the people who abide in Him as branches on the vine.
- **The Exodus and the Wilderness Journey** (Exodus 12-40)  
  The Israelites' journey from slavery in Egypt to the Promised Land prefigures the Church's journey from the slavery of sin to salvation in Christ.
- **The Covenant People of Israel** (Exodus 19:5-6)  
  Israel, as the chosen people in covenant with God, represents the Church, the new people of God through the New Covenant in Christ.
- **The Tabernacle** (Exodus 25-27)  
  The Tabernacle, where God dwelled among His people in the wilderness, is a type of the Church, where God continues to dwell through His presence in the sacraments.
