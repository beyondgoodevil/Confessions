Traditionally, it is believed that **Moses** wrote the Torah, also known as the **Pentateuch** (Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy), during his lifetime, around the **13th–15th century BC**. This was during the time of the **Exodus** of the Israelites from Egypt and their **40 years of wandering in the wilderness**.

The Torah is considered to have been written under **divine inspiration**. It includes laws, historical narratives, and theological teachings attributed to Moses' direct communication with God, especially at **Mount Sinai**, where Moses received the **Ten Commandments** and other instructions for the people of Israel.
