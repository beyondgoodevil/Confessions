### 1. The Virgin Birth:

- **Prophecy**: Isaiah 7:14: “Behold, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 1:22-23 and Luke 1:26-35 describe the miraculous birth of Jesus, conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary.

### 2. The Lineage of the Messiah:

- **Prophecy**: Genesis 12:3 (descendant of Abraham), Genesis 49:10 (from the tribe of Judah), 2 Samuel 7:12-13 (a son of David).
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 1:1-17 and Luke 3:23-38 trace Jesus’ genealogy, showing His descent from Abraham, Judah, and David.

### 3. Born in Bethlehem:

- **Prophecy**: Micah 5:2: “But you, O Bethlehem Ephrathah… from you shall come forth for me one who is to be ruler in Israel.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 2:1-6 and Luke 2:4-7 recount Jesus’ birth in Bethlehem as foretold.

### 4. Herod’s Slaughter of Innocents:

- **Prophecy**: Jeremiah 31:15: “A voice is heard in Ramah, lamentation and bitter weeping... Rachel weeping for her children.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 2:16-18 describes Herod’s order to kill male infants in an attempt to destroy Jesus, fulfilling this prophecy.

### 5. Ministry in Galilee:

- **Prophecy**: Isaiah 9:1-2 foretells a great light shining in Galilee: “The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 4:12-16 shows Jesus beginning His public ministry in Galilee, fulfilling this prophecy.

### 6. Healing and Miracles:

- **Prophecy**: Isaiah 35:5-6: “Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf unstopped; then shall the lame man leap like a deer.”
- **Fulfillment**: Jesus’ ministry was marked by miraculous healings, such as in Matthew 11:4-6, where He tells John the Baptist’s disciples to witness these signs.

### 7. The Triumphal Entry:

- **Prophecy**: Zechariah 9:9: “Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion! … Behold, your king is coming to you; righteous and having salvation, humble and mounted on a donkey.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 21:1-11 and John 12:12-16 describe Jesus’ entry into Jerusalem on a donkey.

### 8. Betrayed for Thirty Pieces of Silver:

- **Prophecy**: Zechariah 11:12-13 foretells the betrayal price and its use to buy a potter’s field.
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 26:14-16 and Matthew 27:3-10 show Judas Iscariot betraying Jesus for thirty pieces of silver, which were later used to buy the potter’s field.

### 9. Suffering and Death:

- **Prophecy**: Isaiah 53 describes the Suffering Servant who bears the sins of others, including details such as being “pierced for our transgressions” and “silent before his accusers.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 26-27, Mark 14-15, and John 18-19 describe Jesus’ passion and crucifixion, fulfilling Isaiah 53 in detail.

### 10. Pierced Hands and Feet:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 22:16: “They have pierced my hands and feet.”
- **Fulfillment**: John 19:18 and John 20:27 recount Jesus’ crucifixion and the wounds in His hands and feet.

### 11. Casting Lots for His Garments:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 22:18: “They divide my garments among them, and for my clothing they cast lots.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 27:35 describes the soldiers casting lots for Jesus’ clothing at His crucifixion.

### 12. No Broken Bones:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 34:20: “He keeps all his bones; not one of them is broken.”
- **Fulfillment**: John 19:31-36 states that Jesus’ legs were not broken on the cross, as was customary, fulfilling this prophecy.

### 13. Burial in a Rich Man’s Tomb:

- **Prophecy**: Isaiah 53:9: “And they made his grave with the wicked and with a rich man in his death.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 27:57-60 describes Jesus being buried in the tomb of Joseph of Arimathea, a wealthy man.

### 14. Resurrection from the Dead:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 16:10: “For you will not abandon my soul to Sheol, or let your holy one see corruption.”
- **Fulfillment**: Acts 2:22-32 and the Gospels proclaim Jesus’ resurrection, fulfilling this prophecy.

### 15. Ascension to Heaven:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 68:18: “You ascended on high, leading a host of captives in your train.”
- **Fulfillment**: Luke 24:50-51 and Acts 1:9-11 describe Jesus’ ascension into heaven.

These fulfilled prophecies demonstrate the divine plan of salvation, where Christ’s life, death, and resurrection complete and reveal the promises given through the prophets of the Old Testament.
