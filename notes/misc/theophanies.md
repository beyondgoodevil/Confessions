Here are some key theophanies in the Old Testament:

1. **The Burning Bush** (Exodus 3:1-6) – God speaks to Moses from a bush that burns but is not consumed.
2. **The Appearance to Abraham** (Genesis 18:1-15) – Three men, one of whom is God, visit Abraham.
3. **Jacob Wrestles with God** (Genesis 32:22-32) – Jacob encounters God in the form of a man.
4. **Pillar of Cloud and Fire** (Exodus 13:21-22) – God leads the Israelites by a cloud and fire.
5. **The Theophany at Mount Sinai** (Exodus 19:16-25) – God descends on the mountain in fire, smoke, and thunder.
6. **The Angel of the Lord and Gideon** (Judges 6:11-24) – God appears to Gideon as the Angel of the Lord.
7. **Isaiah's Vision** (Isaiah 6:1-8) – Isaiah sees God’s glory in the temple.
8. **Fourth Man in the Fiery Furnace** (Daniel 3:8-30) – A divine figure appears with Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in the fire.
9. **God Appears to Elijah** (1 Kings 19:9-18) – God speaks to Elijah in a gentle whisper.
10. **Ezekiel's Vision** (Ezekiel 1:4-28) – Ezekiel sees a vision of God's glory with living creatures and a throne.
11. **God Appears to Samson's Parents** (Judges 13:3-23) – The Angel of the Lord appears to Manoah and his wife, foretelling Samson's birth.
12. **God’s Presence at the Tabernacle** (Exodus 40:34-38) – The cloud of the Lord’s presence fills the tabernacle.
13. **Theophany to Moses in the Cleft of the Rock** (Exodus 33:18-23) – God’s glory passes by Moses, but he only sees His back.
14. **The Vision of the Chariot of Fire** (2 Kings 2:11-12) – Elijah is taken to heaven in a whirlwind, accompanied by chariots and horses of fire.
15. **God Appears to Job** (Job 38-41) – God speaks to Job from a whirlwind, questioning his understanding of creation.
