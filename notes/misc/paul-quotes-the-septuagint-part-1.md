## 1. Romans 3:10–18 — A compilation of LXX quotes

St. Paul strings together multiple Old Testament passages in this section. The key line:

> **“There is none righteous, no, not one…”** — Romans 3:10

This echoes **Psalm 13(14):3 (LXX)**:

> **“There is none that doeth good, no not one.”** — *LXX Psalm 13(14):3*

In the **Masoretic Text**, Psalm 14:3 lacks parts of this line and omits verses that appear in Paul's quote. The LXX includes **additional lines** that are not in the Hebrew, matching Paul's Greek citations almost word for word.

## 2. Romans 9:33

> **“Behold, I lay in Sion a stumbling stone and rock of offense: and whosoever believeth on Him shall not be ashamed.”**

This combines:

- **Isaiah 8:14 (LXX)**: *“a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense”*
- **Isaiah 28:16 (LXX)**: *“He that believes in Him shall not be ashamed”*

However, **Isaiah 28:16** in the **Masoretic Text** says:

> “He that believes shall not make haste,” which is **significantly different**.

Thus, Paul quotes the **LXX version**.

## 3. Hebrews 10:5–7 (often attributed to Paul in Orthodox tradition)

> **“A body hast Thou prepared for Me.”** — Hebrews 10:5  
> Citing **Psalm 39(40):6 (LXX)**:  
> **“Sacrifice and offering Thou wouldest not, but a body hast Thou prepared for Me.”**

The **Masoretic Text** says:

> **“My ears hast Thou opened.”**

This is a **major textual difference**. Paul clearly follows the **LXX**, not the MT.

## 4. 2 Corinthians 6:2

> **“In an acceptable time I have heard thee, and in the day of salvation have I succoured thee.”**

This quotes **Isaiah 49:8 (LXX)** directly.  
The **Masoretic Text** is nearly the same but with nuances in **tense** and **emphasis** that favor the LXX Greek.
