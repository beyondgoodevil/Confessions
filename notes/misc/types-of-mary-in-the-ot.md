### 2. Types of Mary in the Old Testament:

- **Eve** (Genesis 3:15)  
  Mary is the New Eve, as Eve's disobedience led to the fall, but Mary's obedience brings forth the Redeemer. Genesis 3:15 refers to the enmity between the serpent and the woman's seed, symbolically referring to Mary and Christ.
- **The Ark of the Covenant** (Exodus 25:10-22)  
  The Ark, which contained the Word of God (the tablets of the Law), prefigures Mary, who bore the Word made flesh. The Ark was covered in gold and housed God's presence, just as Mary carried Christ.
- **The Burning Bush** (Exodus 3:2)  
  The bush that burns but is not consumed represents Mary, who miraculously bore Christ while remaining a virgin.
- **The Ladder of Jacob** (Genesis 28:12)  
  Jacob's ladder, reaching from earth to heaven, typifies Mary, who is the connection through whom Christ comes into the world.
- **The Queen Mother (Gebirah)** (1 Kings 2:19)  
  In the Davidic Kingdom, the queen was the mother of the king. Mary, as the mother of the King of Kings, is the Queen of Heaven.
- **Judith** (Judith 13:18)  
  Judith, a woman who delivers Israel by slaying the enemy's general, typifies Mary, who delivers humanity through her role in bearing Christ.
- **Hannah** (1 Samuel 1-2)  
  Hannah, who offers her son Samuel to God's service, prefigures Mary offering her son Jesus to fulfill God's plan of salvation.
