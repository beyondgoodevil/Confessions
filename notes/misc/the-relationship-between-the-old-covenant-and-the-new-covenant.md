### Galatians

1. **Abraham as a Type:**
    - **Key Passage:** Galatians 3:6-9, 14
    - **Explanation:** Paul presents Abraham as a model of faith, showing that those who believe in Christ are the true children of Abraham. The promise made to Abraham (Genesis 12:1-3) that all nations would be blessed is fulfilled in Christ, establishing believers as heirs according to the promise.
2. **Law and Promise:**
    - **Key Passage:** Galatians 3:19-25
    - **Explanation:** Paul contrasts the law given through Moses with the promise given to Abraham. The law serves as a "tutor" or guardian, leading to Christ. This typological understanding indicates that the Mosaic Law points to the need for Christ and emphasizes that faith, not law, is the means of justification.
3. **Hagar and Sarah:**
    - **Key Passage:** Galatians 4:21-31
    - **Explanation:** Paul uses the story of Hagar and Sarah to illustrate two covenants: Hagar represents the covenant of the law (Mount Sinai), while Sarah represents the covenant of grace (the promise). This typology emphasizes the freedom found in Christ versus the bondage of the law.

### Hebrews

1. **Christ as the High Priest:**
    - **Key Passage:** Hebrews 4:14-16; 7:1-28
    - **Explanation:** Hebrews presents Christ as the fulfillment of the Levitical priesthood. The priestly order of Melchizedek is introduced as a type, showing that Christ's priesthood is superior and eternal. This typology emphasizes the unique and complete nature of Christ's sacrificial role.
2. **Sacrifice and Atonement:**
    - **Key Passage:** Hebrews 9:11-15
    - **Explanation:** The sacrifices of the Old Testament are seen as types that foreshadow the ultimate sacrifice of Christ. The blood of Christ, offered once for all, fulfills and surpasses the repeated sacrifices of the Old Covenant, providing eternal redemption.
3. **The Tabernacle and the Heavenly Reality:**
    - **Key Passage:** Hebrews 8:1-5; 9:24
    - **Explanation:** The earthly tabernacle and its rituals are types that point to the heavenly reality and Christ's ministry in the true sanctuary. This typology emphasizes that Christ serves in the heavenly realm, interceding for believers.
4. **Covenants:**
    - **Key Passage:** Hebrews 8:6-13
    - **Explanation:** The New Covenant is presented as a fulfillment of the promises made in the Old Covenant. The typological relationship highlights the transition from the old system of law to the new system of grace through Christ.
