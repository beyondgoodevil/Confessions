## 1. Book of the Just (Book of Jashar)

- **Joshua 10:13:**
  *"And the sun stood still, and the moon stopped, until the nation took vengeance on their enemies. Is this not written in the Book of Jashar?"*
- **2 Samuel 1:18:**
  *"And he said it should be taught to the people of Judah; behold, it is written in the Book of Jashar."*

## 2. Book of Nathan the Prophet

- **1 Chronicles 29:29:**
  *"Now the acts of King David, from first to last, are written in the chronicles of Samuel the seer, and in the chronicles of Nathan the prophet, and in the chronicles of Gad the seer."*

## 3. Book of Ahijah the Shilonite

- **2 Chronicles 9:29:**
  *"Now the rest of the acts of Solomon, from first to last, are they not written in the history of Nathan the prophet, and in the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Iddo the seer concerning Jeroboam the son of Nebat?"*

## 4. Book of Iddo (Ado) the Seer

- **2 Chronicles 12:15:**
  *"Now the acts of Rehoboam, from first to last, are they not written in the chronicles of Shemaiah the prophet and of Iddo the seer, according to genealogical records?"*
- **2 Chronicles 13:22:**
  *"And the rest of the acts of Abijah, his ways, and his sayings are written in the story of the prophet Iddo."*

These books are historical sources referenced by the biblical writers, but they are not part of the standard canonical scriptures in any tradition.
