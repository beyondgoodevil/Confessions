The eternal nature of the Melchizedekian priesthood is rooted in **Psalm 110:4**, which states:

*"The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind, 'You are a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek.'"*

This verse is significant in biblical theology because it points to a unique and eternal priesthood distinct from the Levitical priesthood established under the Mosaic Law. Melchizedek, mentioned briefly in **Genesis 14:18-20**, is described as the "king of Salem" and "priest of God Most High," with no recorded genealogy, symbolizing an eternal and divine appointment.

The New Testament, particularly in **Hebrews 7**, provides further context. The author of Hebrews explains that Jesus fulfills this prophecy, holding a priesthood not based on lineage (as in the Levitical order) but on the "power of an indestructible life" (**Hebrews 7:16**). For example:

- **Hebrews 7:21**: *“The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind: ‘You are a priest forever.’”*
- **Hebrews 7:24**: Jesus' priesthood is described as unchangeable and eternal because He lives forever.

Thus, the Melchizedekian priesthood, unlike the temporary Levitical priesthood, is eternal, perfect, and fulfilled in Jesus Christ.
