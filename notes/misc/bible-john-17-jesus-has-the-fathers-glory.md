## Jesus Prays for Himself

- **17:1** – Jesus lifts His eyes to heaven and says that the hour has come: "Glorify Your Son, that Your Son also may glorify You."
- **17:2** – The Father has given Him authority over all flesh, that He should give eternal life to as many as the Father has given Him.
- **17:3** – Eternal life is to know the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom He has sent.
- **17:4** – He has glorified the Father on earth and finished the work given Him to do.
- **17:5** – He asks the Father to glorify Him together with Himself, with the glory He had with the Father before the world was.
