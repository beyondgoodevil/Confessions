## Types of Christ in the Old Testament

- **Adam** (Romans 5:14)  
  Adam is a type of Christ as the first man through whom humanity fell, just as Christ is the "Second Adam" who redeems humanity.
- **Melchizedek** (Genesis 14:18-20)  
  The mysterious priest-king who blesses Abraham and offers bread and wine prefigures Christ as the eternal High Priest (Hebrews 7).
- **Isaac** (Genesis 22:1-14)  
  Isaac, Abraham's only son who is nearly sacrificed, typifies Christ as the only Son of God, sacrificed for our sins.
- **Joseph** (Genesis 37-50)  
  Joseph, who is rejected by his brothers and later saves them, is a type of Christ, rejected by His people but later becoming the Savior.
- **Moses** (Exodus 2-34)  
  Moses, as the deliverer of Israel from slavery and mediator of the Old Covenant, prefigures Christ, the Mediator of the New Covenant.
- **David** (2 Samuel 7:12-16)  
  David, the shepherd king, foreshadows Christ, the Good Shepherd and eternal King from David's line.
- **Jonah** (Jonah 1-3, Matthew 12:40)  
  Jonah, who spends three days in the belly of a fish and then returns to life, prefigures Christ's death, burial, and resurrection after three days.
- **The Bronze Serpent** (Numbers 21:9, John 3:14-15)  
  The bronze serpent lifted up in the wilderness to heal those bitten by snakes typifies Christ lifted up on the cross to heal humanity from sin.
- **The Passover Lamb** (Exodus 12, 1 Corinthians 5:7)  
  The spotless lamb whose blood saved the Israelites from death is a clear type of Christ, the Lamb of God, whose blood saves us.
- **The Manna from Heaven** (Exodus 16, John 6:31-35)  
  Manna, the bread from heaven, prefigures Christ, the true Bread of Life.
