## Early Canon Lists

1. **Muratorian Canon** - c. 170-200 AD
2. **Antioch Canon** - c. 264 AD
3. **Laodicean Canon** - c. 363-364 AD
4. **Hippo Canon** - 393 AD
5. **Carthaginian Canon** - 397 AD
6. **Canon of Eusebius** - c. 330 AD
7. **Apostolic Constitutions** - c. 380 AD

## Other Notable Mentions

While not formal canons, the following lists reflect early recognition of certain texts:

- **Clement of Alexandria's Writings** - c. 200 AD
- **Tertullian's References** - c. 155-240 AD

These represent the various canons and significant lists that emerged in the early Church, illustrating the diverse perspectives on Scripture before the formal establishment of the biblical canon.
