title: Number of Books in the Bible in Different Denominations
tags: [theology, canon]
summary: Old and New Testament book counts for Catholic, Orthodox, Protestant, Anglican, Lutheran and Reformed churches.
---

## 1. Roman Catholic Church

- **Total Books:** 73
    - **Old Testament:** 46 (including the Deuterocanonical books)
    - **New Testament:** 27

## 2. Eastern Orthodox Church

- **Total Books:** 76 to 81 (varies by jurisdiction)
    - **Old Testament:** 49 to 55 (including additional Deuterocanonical books)
    - **New Testament:** 27

## 3. Protestant Churches

- **Total Books:** 66
    - **Old Testament:** 39
    - **New Testament:** 27

## 4. Anglican Communion

- **Total Books:** 66 (similar to Protestant Bibles) but may also recognize the Apocrypha as useful for reading.
    - **Old Testament:** 39
    - **New Testament:** 27

## 5. Lutheran Churches

- **Total Books:** 66 (similar to Protestant Bibles)
    - **Old Testament:** 39
    - **New Testament:** 27
- Note: Some Lutherans may include the Apocrypha in their editions, which would bring the total to 73.

## 6. Reformed Churches

- **Total Books:** 66
    - **Old Testament:** 39
    - **New Testament:** 27
