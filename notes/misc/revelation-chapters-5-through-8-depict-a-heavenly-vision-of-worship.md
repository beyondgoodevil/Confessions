### Key Verses Depicting Worship:

**Revelation 5:**

- **Verses 8-10**: The four living creatures and the 24 elders worship the Lamb with harps and golden bowls of incense (representing prayers of the saints).  
  *"And they sang a new song, saying:  
  'Worthy are You to take the scroll and to open its seals, for You were slain, and by Your blood You ransomed people for God from every tribe and language and people and nation, and You have made them a kingdom and priests to our God, and they shall reign on the earth.'"*
- **Verses 11-12**: An innumerable host of angels joins the worship, praising the Lamb:  
  *"Worthy is the Lamb who was slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and might and honor and glory and blessing!"*
- **Verses 13-14**: All creation joins in praise:  
  *"To Him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb be blessing and honor and glory and might forever and ever!"*

---

**Revelation 6:**

This chapter describes the opening of the first six seals, revealing judgments upon the earth. While worship is not directly described here, the narrative demonstrates the Lamb's authority to execute God's plan.

---

**Revelation 7:**

- **Verses 9-12**: A great multitude from all nations, dressed in white robes, stands before the throne and the Lamb, worshiping God:  
  *"Salvation belongs to our God who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!"*  
  Angels, elders, and living creatures join in praise: *"Amen! Blessing and glory and wisdom and thanksgiving and honor and power and might be to our God forever and ever! Amen."*
- **Verses 13-17**: The multitude is identified as those who have come out of the great tribulation, and their presence is described as eternal worship and service to God:  
  *"They are before the throne of God, and serve Him day and night in His temple; and He who sits on the throne will shelter them with His presence."*

---

**Revelation 8:**

- **Verses 3-4**: Worship includes the offering of incense mixed with the prayers of the saints:  
  *"And the smoke of the incense, with the prayers of the saints, rose before God from the hand of the angel."*

This scene illustrates how the prayers of believers are integrated into the worship and heavenly liturgy.

---

### Themes of Worship:

1. **The Centrality of the Lamb**: Jesus, the Lamb, is worshiped alongside God the Father, emphasizing His divine authority and role in salvation.
2. **Liturgical Imagery**: Heavenly worship involves symbolic acts such as singing, the use of incense, and prostration.
3. **Universal Praise**: Worship encompasses angels, elders, living creatures, and all creation, showcasing the universality of God's glory.
4. **Prayers of the Saints**: Earthly prayers are part of the heavenly worship, reflecting a connection between the Church on earth and the heavenly liturgy.

These chapters highlight that worship is central to the life of the Church, both in heaven and on earth, aligning with the Eastern Orthodox understanding of liturgical worship as a participation in heavenly realities.
