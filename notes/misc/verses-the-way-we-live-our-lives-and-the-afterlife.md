### 1. Matthew 16:27

"For the Son of Man will come in the glory of His Father with His angels, and then He will reward each according to his works."

- **Significance:** This verse underscores that Jesus will evaluate individuals based on their deeds, indicating that our actions in this life will influence our eternal rewards.

### 2. 2 Corinthians 5:10

"For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive the things done in the body, according to what he has done, whether good or bad."

- **Significance:** Paul explains that all will face judgment and will receive recompense based on their earthly actions, highlighting the importance of living a righteous life.

### 3. Galatians 6:7-8

"Do not be deceived: God is not mocked; for whatever a man sows, that he will also reap. For he who sows to his flesh will of the flesh reap corruption, but he who sows to the Spirit will of the Spirit reap everlasting life."

- **Significance:** This passage emphasizes the principle of sowing and reaping, reinforcing that our choices in life directly impact our spiritual outcomes.

### 4. James 2:17-18

"Thus also faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead. But someone will say, 'You have faith, and I have works.' Show me your faith without your works, and I will show you my faith by my works."

- **Significance:** James stresses that genuine faith is demonstrated through works, implying that our spiritual state is reflected in our actions and that these have eternal implications.

### 5. 1 Peter 1:17

"And if you call on the Father, who without partiality judges according to each one's work, conduct yourselves throughout the time of your stay here in fear."

- **Significance:** Peter reminds believers that God judges impartially based on works, encouraging a life of reverence and responsibility during our earthly existence.

### 6. Revelation 20:12-13

"And I saw the dead, small and great, standing before God, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the Book of Life. And the dead were judged according to their works, by the things which were written in the books."

- **Significance:** This vision of judgment indicates that our earthly deeds are recorded and will be evaluated, reinforcing the link between our lives and the afterlife.

### 7. Matthew 25:31-46 (The Parable of the Sheep and the Goats)

"Then the King will say to those on His right hand, 'Come, you blessed of My Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world: for I was hungry and you gave Me food; I was thirsty and you gave Me drink; I was a stranger and you took Me in; I was naked and you clothed Me; I was sick and you visited Me; I was in prison and you came to Me.' ... 'And these will go away into everlasting punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.'"

- **Significance:** This parable emphasizes that acts of kindness and mercy toward others are integral to inheriting the Kingdom, illustrating that our treatment of others affects our eternal destiny.
