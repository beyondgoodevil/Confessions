title: Jesus fulfills the covenant promises made to Abraham
tags: [theology, covenant, scripture]
summary: Four passages from Galatians on Christ as the fulfilment of the promises to Abraham.
---

1. **Galatians 3:13-14:**  
   "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree: That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith."  
   This passage indicates that Christ's redemptive work fulfills the covenant promises, allowing both Jews and Gentiles to receive the blessings of Abraham.
2. **Galatians 3:16:**  
   "Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ."  
   Here, Paul explicitly states that the promises were made to Christ, affirming that He is the fulfillment of the covenant.
3. **Galatians 4:4-5:**  
   "But when the fullness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law, To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons."  
   This verse indicates that Jesus came to fulfill the law and the covenant, enabling believers to become children of God.
4. **Galatians 4:24-26:**  
   "Which things are an allegory: for these are the two covenants; the one from the mount Sinai, which gendereth to bondage, which is Agar. But Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all."  
   This contrast between the two covenants illustrates how Jesus brings freedom and fulfillment that the old covenant could not provide.

These passages collectively affirm that Jesus is the fulfillment of the covenant promises made to Abraham, providing redemption and the promise of the Holy Spirit to all who believe in Him.
