title: Galatians regarding the descent or lineage of Christ
tags: [theology, scripture, covenant]
summary: Three verses from Galatians on Christ as Abraham's seed, born of a woman.
---

1. **Galatians 3:16:**  
   "Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ."  
   This verse highlights that the promises made to Abraham specifically pertain to Christ as his singular descendant.
2. **Galatians 4:4:**  
   "But when the fullness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law."  
   Here, Paul underscores the humanity of Christ, emphasizing that He was born of a woman, indicating His human descent.
3. **Galatians 3:29:**  
   "And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise."  
   This verse connects believers in Christ to the covenant made with Abraham, highlighting the significance of descent from him.
