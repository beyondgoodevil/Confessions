### Isaiah Called to Be a Prophet

- **6:1** (highlighted) – In the year King Uzziah died, Isaiah says ==I saw the Lord== sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, the train of His robe filling the temple.
- **6:2** – Seraphim stand above it, each with six wings: two covering the face, two the feet, and two for flying.
- **6:3** – They cry to one another, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory!"
- **6:4** – The doorposts shake at the voice, and the house fills with smoke.
- **6:5** – Isaiah says, "Woe is me, for I am undone": a man of unclean lips among a people of unclean lips, whose eyes have seen the King, the Lord of hosts.
