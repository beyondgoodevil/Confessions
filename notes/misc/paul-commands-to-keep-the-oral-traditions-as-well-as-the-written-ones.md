---
title: Paul commands to keep the oral traditions as well as the written ones
tags: [theology, tradition, scripture]
summary: 2 Thessalonians 2:15 and 1 Corinthians 11:2 on holding to traditions delivered by word and by letter.
---

### 1. 2 Thessalonians 2:15:

*"So then, brothers, stand firm and hold to the traditions that you were taught by us, either by our spoken word or by our letter."*

Here, Paul explicitly tells the Thessalonians to hold fast to the traditions passed on both through **oral teaching** and written letters.

### 2. 1 Corinthians 11:2:

*"Now I commend you because you remember me in everything and maintain the traditions even as I delivered them to you."*

In this passage, Paul praises the Corinthians for **maintaining the traditions** that he delivered to them, which would have included both oral and written teachings.
