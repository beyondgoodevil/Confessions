### Jesus Transfigured on the Mount

- **17:1** – After six days Jesus takes Peter, James and John up a high mountain by themselves.
- **17:2** (highlighted) – He is transfigured before them: His face shines like the sun and His clothes become as white as the ==light==.
- **17:3** – Moses and Elijah appear, talking with Him.
- **17:4** – Peter offers to make three tabernacles: for Jesus, Moses and Elijah.
- **17:5** – A bright cloud overshadows them and a voice says, "This is My beloved Son... Hear Him!"
- **17:6-8** – The disciples fall on their faces in fear; Jesus touches them and tells them not to be afraid. Looking up, they see Jesus only.
- **17:9** – Coming down, Jesus commands them to tell no one the vision until the Son of Man is risen from the dead.
- **17:10-13** – Asked why the scribes say Elijah must come first, Jesus answers that Elijah has come already and was not recognised, and that the Son of Man will likewise suffer. The disciples understand He means John the Baptist.
