The Messiah is first of all a **king**: "Christ" (*Christos*, *Mashiach*) means "anointed", and kings were the ones anointed. The theme runs from God as Israel's King, through David's line, to the cross with "King of the Jews" written over it, and the King of kings in Revelation. Verses KJV.

## 1. God Is the King

- Exod 15:18: "**The LORD shall reign for ever and ever**." The song at the Red Sea.
- 1 Sam 8:7: when Israel asks for a king, God tells Samuel: "they have not rejected thee, but **they have rejected me, that I should not reign over them**."
- Ps 47:7: "For **God is the King of all the earth**: sing ye praises with understanding."
- Ps 93:1: "**The LORD reigneth**, he is clothed with majesty."
- Isa 6:5: "mine eyes have seen **the King, the LORD of hosts**." John 12:41 says Isaiah saw *Christ's* glory there.

So the human king was always meant to stand in for God's own kingship. The Messiah unites the two: the divine King and the son of David.

## 2. Melchizedek: Priest and King

- Gen 14:18: "And **Melchizedek king of Salem** brought forth bread and wine: and **he was the priest of the most high God**."
- Heb 7:1-2: "first being by interpretation **King of righteousness**, and after that also **King of Salem, which is, King of peace**."
- In Israel king and priest were kept separate (Uzziah was struck with leprosy for offering incense, 2 Chr 26:18-19). Christ is both, after Melchizedek's order. See [[The Melchizedekian Priesthood]].

## 3. The Promise to David

- Gen 49:10: "**The sceptre shall not depart from Judah**, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, **until Shiloh come**; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be."
- Num 24:17: "there shall come **a Star out of Jacob**, and **a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel**."
- 2 Sam 7:12-13, 16: "I will set up thy seed after thee... and **I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever**... thine house and thy kingdom shall be established for ever before thee: **thy throne shall be established for ever**."
- Ps 89:3-4, 36: "I have sworn unto David my servant, Thy seed will I establish for ever... **His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me**."
- Jer 23:5: "I will raise unto David **a righteous Branch**, and **a King shall reign and prosper**."

## 4. The Royal Psalms

- **Ps 2:6-7**: "Yet have **I set my king upon my holy hill of Zion**... **Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee**." Quoted of Christ in Acts 13:33, Heb 1:5.
- **Ps 2:8-9**: "I shall give thee **the heathen for thine inheritance**... Thou shalt break them with a rod of iron." Rev 2:27, 12:5, 19:15.
- **Ps 45:6-7**: "**Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever**: the sceptre of thy kingdom is a right sceptre... therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee." Heb 1:8-9 applies it to the Son: the King is addressed as God.
- **Ps 72:8, 11**: "He shall have dominion also from sea to sea... Yea, **all kings shall fall down before him: all nations shall serve him**." The Magi bring gifts (72:10, "the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts").
- **Ps 110:1**: "The LORD said unto my Lord, **Sit thou at my right hand**, until I make thine enemies thy footstool." The OT verse most quoted in the NT. Jesus uses it to show the Messiah is more than David's son (Matt 22:41-45). See [[Christ's Ascension: NT and Psalm 110]].
- **Ps 24:7-10**: "Lift up your heads, O ye gates... and **the King of glory shall come in**. Who is this King of glory? The LORD strong and mighty." Sung at the Ascension and on Palm Sunday.

## 5. The Prophets' Coming King

- Isa 9:6-7: "unto us a child is born... **the government shall be upon his shoulder**... **The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace**. Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, **upon the throne of David**."
- Isa 11:1: "there shall come forth **a rod out of the stem of Jesse**."
- Mic 5:2: "But thou, **Beth-lehem**... out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be **ruler in Israel**; whose goings forth have been **from of old, from everlasting**."
- Zech 9:9: "**behold, thy King cometh unto thee**: he is just, and having salvation; **lowly, and riding upon an ass**." Fulfilled on Palm Sunday (Matt 21:5).
- Ezek 34:23-24: "I will set up **one shepherd** over them... **my servant David**." Written long after David died: the coming David. King as shepherd.

## 6. Daniel 7: The Son of Man

- Dan 2:44: "the God of heaven **set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed**." The stone not cut with hands.
- Dan 7:13-14: "one like **the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven**, and came to the Ancient of days... And there was given him **dominion, and glory, and a kingdom**, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is **an everlasting dominion**."
- Jesus' favourite title for Himself is "Son of man", and at His trial He quotes this: "ye shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of power, and **coming in the clouds of heaven**" (Matt 26:64). The high priest tears his clothes: blasphemy. He'd claimed Ps 110 and Dan 7 together.

## 7. The Gospels: A Strange King

- Matt 1:1: "The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, **the son of David**."
- Luke 1:32-33: "the Lord God shall give unto him **the throne of his father David**: And he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever; and **of his kingdom there shall be no end**." Echoes 2 Sam 7.
- Matt 2:2: the Magi: "**Where is he that is born King of the Jews?**"
- Mark 1:15: "the time is fulfilled, and **the kingdom of God is at hand**."
- John 18:36-37: "**My kingdom is not of this world**... Thou sayest that **I am a king**. To this end was I born."
- The Passion as a mock coronation:
    - Matt 27:28-29: **scarlet robe**, **crown of thorns**, **reed** for a sceptre, "**Hail, King of the Jews!**"
    - John 19:19: the title over the cross, "**JESUS OF NAZARETH THE KING OF THE JEWS**", in Hebrew, Greek and Latin.
    - John 19:15: "We have **no king but Caesar**." (Cf. 1 Sam 8:7.)
- Luke 23:42: the thief: "Lord, remember me when thou comest **into thy kingdom**." The first to see the cross as a throne.

## 8. Enthroned

- Matt 28:18: "**All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth**."
- Eph 1:20-22: "set him at his own right hand in the heavenly places, **far above all principality, and power**... and hath **put all things under his feet**."
- Phil 2:9-11: "God also hath highly exalted him... that at the name of Jesus **every knee should bow**... and that **every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord**." See [[The Name of God]].
- 1 Cor 15:25: "For **he must reign, till he hath put all enemies under his feet**." The last enemy is death (15:26).
- Rev 1:5: "**the prince of the kings of the earth**."
- Rev 11:15: "**The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ**; and he shall reign for ever and ever."
- Rev 19:16: "And he hath on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, **KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS**."

## 9. Notes

*Mine.*

- The two threads (God is King; David's son will be King) meet in Christ, who is both. Ps 45 and Ps 110 already hint at it: the King is addressed as God; David calls his son "Lord".
- The cross is the enthronement, in John especially: lifted up (John 12:32), crowned, titled, proclaimed in three languages. The Orthodox hymn for the Exaltation of the Cross: "O Lord, save Thy people... granting victory to Thy people."
- The Orthodox icon of Christ **Pantocrator** ("Ruler of All") is this theme painted.
- Christ's people share the kingship: "kings and priests unto God" (Rev 1:6; 1 Pet 2:9; Exod 19:6).
