Several passages in the Old Testament are understood to reveal the Angel of the Lord as a pre-incarnate manifestation of Jesus Christ, the Second Person of the Trinity. Below are key instances where this is believed to occur, based on theological interpretation and patristic writings:

## 1. Hagar's Encounter (Genesis 16:7-13)

- **Context**: Hagar flees from Sarah and encounters the Angel of the Lord.
- **Key Verse**: *"So she called the name of the Lord who spoke to her, 'You are the God who sees me.'"*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel identifies with God and speaks with divine authority.

## 2. The Sacrifice of Isaac (Genesis 22:11-18)

- **Context**: Abraham prepares to sacrifice Isaac.
- **Key Verse**: *"The Angel of the Lord called to him from heaven, 'Do not lay your hand on the boy…'"*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel confirms God's covenant blessings, acting as God Himself.

## 3. Burning Bush (Exodus 3:2-6)

- **Context**: Moses encounters the burning bush.
- **Key Verse**: *"The Angel of the Lord appeared to him in a flame of fire… God called to him out of the bush."*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel reveals God's holy name, affirming divine identity.

## 4. Pillar of Cloud and Fire (Exodus 13:21; 14:19)

- **Context**: The Israelites are led through the wilderness.
- **Key Verse**: *"The Angel of God, who was going before the host of Israel, moved and went behind them."*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel leads and protects, acting as a divine guide.

## 5. Balaam's Donkey (Numbers 22:22-35)

- **Context**: The Angel of the Lord confronts Balaam.
- **Key Verse**: *"The Angel of the Lord said to him, 'Behold, I have come out to oppose you.'"*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel acts with divine authority to judge and correct.

## 6. Joshua’s Vision (Joshua 5:13-15)

- **Context**: Joshua encounters the commander of the Lord’s army.
- **Key Verse**: *"Take off your sandals, for the place where you are standing is holy."*
- **Theological Insight**: Many interpret this figure as the Angel of the Lord, symbolizing divine presence.

## 7. Gideon’s Call (Judges 6:11-24)

- **Context**: Gideon receives his mission from the Angel of the Lord.
- **Key Verse**: *"When Gideon perceived that he was the Angel of the Lord, he said, 'Alas, O Lord God! For now I have seen the Angel of the Lord face to face.'"*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel accepts worship and speaks as God.

## 8. Samson’s Birth Announcement (Judges 13:3-22)

- **Context**: The Angel of the Lord announces Samson’s birth.
- **Key Verse**: *"The Angel of the Lord ascended in the flame of the altar. Then Manoah knew that he was the Angel of the Lord."*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel is recognized as divine and elicits fear of seeing God.

## 9. David’s Census Punishment (2 Samuel 24:16 / 1 Chronicles 21:15-17)

- **Context**: The Angel of the Lord executes judgment on Israel.
- **Key Verse**: *"The Angel of the Lord was by the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."*
- **Theological Insight**: David appeals to the Angel as though addressing God directly.

## 10. Elijah’s Journey (1 Kings 19:5-7)

- **Context**: The Angel provides for Elijah in the wilderness.
- **Key Verse**: *"The Angel of the Lord came again a second time and touched him and said, 'Arise and eat.'"*
- **Theological Insight**: The Angel’s care mirrors divine sustenance.

## Key Themes in Eastern Orthodox Theology

In these passages, the Angel of the Lord:

- **Speaks with God’s Authority**: Often speaks in the first person as God.
- **Receives Worship**: Actions such as Gideon and Manoah offering sacrifices indicate divine identity.
- **Reveals Divine Will**: Plays a central role in God’s covenants and missions.

These instances affirm the Church’s belief in Christ’s eternal existence and His active role in the Old Testament.
