The image quotes each verse in a modern copyrighted translation, so the verses are outlined here.

## 1. Genesis 12:7

- **The verse:** the Lord appears to Abram and promises the land to his offspring; Abram builds an altar there to the Lord who had appeared to him.
- **Location**: Shechem, after God's promise of the land to his descendants.

## 2. Genesis 12:8

- **The verse:** he moves on to the hills east of Bethel and pitches his tent, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east. There he builds an altar to the Lord and calls on the name of the Lord.

## 3. Genesis 13:18

- **The verse:** Abram goes to live near the great trees of Mamre at Hebron, pitches his tents, and builds an altar to the Lord there.

## 4. Genesis 22:9

- **The verse:** at the place God had told him of, Abraham builds an altar, arranges the wood, binds his son Isaac, and lays him on the altar on top of the wood.
- **Location**: Mount Moriah, where Abraham was tested with the sacrifice of Isaac.

These instances mark significant moments in Abraham's journey of faith and his covenant relationship with God.
