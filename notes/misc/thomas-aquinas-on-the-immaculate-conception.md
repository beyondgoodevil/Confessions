title: Thomas Aquinas on the Immaculate Conception
tags: [theology, thomism, mary]
summary: Aquinas did not hold that Mary had to be preserved from original sin at her conception.
---

Thomas Aquinas discussed the Immaculate Conception in his *Summa Theologica*. Specifically, in **Part III, Question 27, Article 2**, Aquinas addresses the question of whether it was fitting for the Blessed Virgin Mary to be conceived without original sin. He concludes that, while Mary was pure and sinless in her life, he did not think it necessary for her to be preserved from original sin from the moment of her conception, as he held that Christ’s redemptive work would cleanse her at the time of conception.

Here is a quote from his work:

> "It was fitting that she should be free from all sin, but not necessary for her to be free from original sin at her conception."

This position was in line with the theological context of his time, where belief in the Immaculate Conception was not yet formally defined by the Church. The doctrine was later defined in 1854 by Pope Pius IX.
