The image is a screenshot of an NPR news article (Religion section, 15 February 2022, by Rachel Treisman). The article is copyrighted, so this note summarises it rather than copying it.

- A Catholic priest in Arizona, the Rev. Andres Arango, resigned after the Diocese of Phoenix found he had used the wrong wording in baptisms up until 17 June 2021.
- He said "we baptize you" where the formula requires "I baptize you". The diocese ruled every baptism performed with the wrong word invalid.
- The diocese's reasoning: it is Christ, not the community, who baptizes, so the wording matters.
- A diocese spokesperson said he is believed to have used the wording since his ordination in 1995, and that those affected number in the thousands.
- Bishop Thomas J. Olmsted said the finding was confirmed in consultation with the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, which had affirmed in 2020 that baptisms using "We baptize you" are not valid.
