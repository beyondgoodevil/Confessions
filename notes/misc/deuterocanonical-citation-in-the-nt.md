## 1. Wisdom of Solomon

- **New Testament Reference:**
    - **Matthew 5:22:** Jesus mentions the concept of judgment, echoing Wisdom 1:5-6, which emphasizes the idea of divine judgment.
    - **Romans 1:20-23:** Paul discusses the folly of idolatry, reflecting the wisdom found in Wisdom 13 regarding the recognition of God through creation.

## 2. Sirach (Ecclesiasticus)

- **New Testament Reference:**
    - **Matthew 7:12:** The Golden Rule resembles teachings in Sirach 31:15 and Sirach 35:1, which speak of treating others with kindness and respect.
    - **James 1:19:** The exhortation to be quick to listen and slow to speak can be related to Sirach 5:11-12, which advises against hasty speech.

## 3. Tobit

- **New Testament Reference:**
    - **Matthew 6:1-4:** The teaching on almsgiving relates to Tobit 4:7-11, emphasizing the importance of giving to the poor and doing righteous deeds.

## 4. 2 Maccabees

- **New Testament Reference:**
    - **Hebrews 11:35:** The mention of women receiving their dead back to life echoes the story of the mother and her seven sons in 2 Maccabees 7, who were martyred for their faith.

## 5. Baruch

- **New Testament Reference:**
    - **Matthew 5:6:** The Beatitudes express similar sentiments to the teachings found in Baruch 4:4, which emphasizes the importance of seeking wisdom.

## 6. Additions to Daniel

- **New Testament Reference:**
    - **Matthew 24:15:** The reference to the "abomination of desolation" can be tied to the themes found in the Additions to Daniel, specifically in the context of faithful resistance against persecution.
