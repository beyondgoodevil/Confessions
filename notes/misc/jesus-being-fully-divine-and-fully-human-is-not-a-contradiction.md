---
title: Jesus being fully divine and fully human is not a contradiction
tags: [theology, christology, apologetics]
summary: Why the excluded middle does not rule out two natures in one Person.
---

Even if we apply the law of the excluded middle, this law applies to contradictions within the same category (e.g., a being cannot be both mortal and immortal in the same way). However, in Christ, the divine and human natures exist without confusion, change, division, or separation (Chalcedonian Definition), meaning they do not negate each other. He is fully divine by nature and fully human by nature but remains one divine Person (hypostasis).

Furthermore, the distinction between **nature** and **person** is crucial. Nature refers to **what** something is (essence), while person refers to **who** someone is (hypostasis). If there were no real distinction between nature and person, then all human persons would collapse into one being, since we all share the same human nature. This would render individual personhood meaningless, demonstrating the necessity of the distinction.
