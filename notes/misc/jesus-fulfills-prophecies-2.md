### 7. The Triumphal Entry:

- **Prophecy**: Zechariah 9:9: “Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion! … Behold, your king is coming to you; righteous and having salvation, humble and mounted on a donkey.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 21:1-11 and John 12:12-16 describe Jesus’ entry into Jerusalem on a donkey.

### 8. Betrayed for Thirty Pieces of Silver:

- **Prophecy**: Zechariah 11:12-13 foretells the betrayal price and its use to buy a potter’s field.
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 26:14-16 and Matthew 27:3-10 show Judas Iscariot betraying Jesus for thirty pieces of silver, which were later used to buy the potter’s field.

### 9. Suffering and Death:

- **Prophecy**: Isaiah 53 describes the Suffering Servant who bears the sins of others, including details such as being “pierced for our transgressions” and “silent before his accusers.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 26-27, Mark 14-15, and John 18-19 describe Jesus’ passion and crucifixion, fulfilling Isaiah 53 in detail.

### 10. Pierced Hands and Feet:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 22:16: “They have pierced my hands and feet.”
- **Fulfillment**: John 19:18 and John 20:27 recount Jesus’ crucifixion and the wounds in His hands and feet.

### 11. Casting Lots for His Garments:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 22:18: “They divide my garments among them, and for my clothing they cast lots.”
- **Fulfillment**: Matthew 27:35 describes the soldiers casting lots for Jesus’ clothing at His crucifixion.

### 12. No Broken Bones:

- **Prophecy**: Psalm 34:20: “He keeps all his bones; not one of them is broken.”
- **Fulfillment**: John 19:31-36 states that Jesus’ legs were not broken on the cross, as was customary, fulfilling this prophecy.
