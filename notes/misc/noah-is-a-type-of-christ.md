---
title: Noah is a type of Christ
tags: [theology, covenant, scripture]
summary: Ten parallels between Noah and Christ.
---

1. **Deliverance through Water:** Noah's ark provides deliverance from the flood, which represents judgment. Similarly, Christ delivers humanity from sin and death through His crucifixion and resurrection, which can be seen as a new creation, emerging from the waters of baptism.
2. **A New Beginning:** After the flood, God establishes a new covenant with Noah, signifying a fresh start for humanity. This mirrors the new covenant established through Christ, who brings about the renewal of humanity through His sacrifice and the gift of the Holy Spirit.
3. **Faithfulness and Obedience:** Noah is characterized by his unwavering faith and obedience to God's command to build the ark. This parallels Christ's perfect obedience to the Father's will, even unto death, showing that both figures exemplify faithfulness and trust in God's plan for salvation.
4. **Priestly Role:** Noah acts as a priestly figure, offering sacrifices after the flood (Genesis 8:20). In a similar manner, Christ is the ultimate High Priest who offers Himself as the perfect sacrifice for the sins of the world.
5. **Preservation of Life:** Noah preserves his family and the animals, ensuring the continuation of life on Earth. Likewise, Christ preserves eternal life for believers, granting them the promise of resurrection and eternal communion with God.
6. **Covenant Establishment:** God establishes a covenant with Noah (Genesis 9:8-17) that serves as a foundation for future covenants. In Christ, the New Covenant fulfills and transcends the earlier covenants, offering salvation to all of humanity.
7. **Symbol of Salvation:** The ark symbolizes salvation through faith. Just as those who entered the ark were saved from destruction, those who put their faith in Christ are saved from eternal separation from God.
8. **Suffering and Righteousness:** Noah endured mockery and scorn while building the ark, akin to the suffering Christ faced during His ministry and crucifixion. Both figures embody righteousness amid a corrupt world.
9. **Sign of Hope:** The rainbow, God's sign to Noah, represents hope and mercy after judgment. Christ's resurrection serves as the ultimate sign of hope for believers, promising victory over sin and death.
10. **Prophetic Role:** Noah is described as a "preacher of righteousness" (2 Peter 2:5), proclaiming God's warning of impending judgment. Similarly, Christ is the ultimate prophet who speaks God's truth and calls people to repentance, offering salvation through His life and teachings.
