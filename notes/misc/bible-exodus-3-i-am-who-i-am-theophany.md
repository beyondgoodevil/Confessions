## Moses at the Burning Bush

- **3:1** – Moses, tending the flock of Jethro, comes to Horeb, the mountain of God.
- **3:2-3** – The Angel of the Lord appears to him in a flame of fire from a bush that burns but is not consumed. Moses turns aside to look.
- **3:4** – God calls to him from the bush, "Moses, Moses!" He answers, "Here ==I am==."
- **3:5-6** – He is told to take off his sandals, for the ground is holy. The speaker says, "==I am== the God of your father—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob." Moses hides his face, afraid to look upon God.
- **3:7-10** – The Lord has seen the oppression of His people in Egypt and has come down to deliver them into a land flowing with milk and honey. He sends Moses to Pharaoh.
- **3:11-12** – Moses asks who he is to go. God answers that He will be with him, and that the sign will be that the people serve God on this mountain.
- **3:13** – Moses asks what to say when the people ask God's name.
- **3:14** – God says, "==I AM== WHO ==I AM==," and that Moses is to say, "==I AM== has sent me to you."
- **3:15** – He is to say that the Lord God of their fathers has sent him; this is His name forever and His memorial to all generations.
- **3:16-18** – Moses is to gather the elders, tell them God has visited them, and go with them to ask the king of Egypt for three days' journey into the wilderness to sacrifice.
- **3:19-20** – God says, "==I am== sure" the king will not let them go, and that He will strike Egypt with wonders, after which he will let them go.
- **3:21-22** – The people will not leave empty-handed; they will be given silver, gold and clothing by the Egyptians.
