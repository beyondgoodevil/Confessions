---
title: Overview of where Jesus refers to oral tradition
tags: [theology, tradition, scripture]
summary: Six Gospel passages in which Jesus refers to or relies on oral tradition.
---

### 1. Mark 7:8-9

*"You leave the commandment of God and hold to the tradition of men."*  
In this passage, Jesus criticizes the Pharisees for prioritizing human traditions over God's commandments, highlighting the existence of oral traditions that were passed down.

### 2. Matthew 15:3

*"And he answered them, 'And why do you break the commandment of God for the sake of your tradition?'"*  
Here, Jesus challenges the Pharisees by pointing out how their oral traditions sometimes contradicted God's commandments, indicating that they placed greater emphasis on human traditions than on the divine law.

### 3. Matthew 23:2-3

*"The scribes and the Pharisees sit on Moses' seat, so practice and observe whatever they tell you—but not what they do. For they preach, but do not practice."*  
This passage highlights the authority of the Pharisees in teaching the Law, which included oral traditions alongside the written Torah.

### 4. Luke 10:25-28

In the parable of the Good Samaritan, Jesus engages in a discussion about the Law, which was informed by both written and oral traditions. The lawyer references the commandment to love one's neighbor, a principle rooted in the broader context of Jewish law, which included oral interpretations.

### 5. John 7:22-23

*"Moses gave you circumcision (not that it is from Moses, but from the fathers), and you circumcise a man on the Sabbath."*  
Here, Jesus refers to the practice of circumcision, which was part of the oral tradition passed down through generations, indicating the continuity of Jewish customs.

### 6. John 21:25

*"Now there are also many other things that Jesus did. Were every one of them to be written, I suppose that the world itself could not contain the books that would be written."*  
This verse suggests that much of Jesus' teachings and actions were transmitted orally, implying that not all His teachings were captured in written form.
