Being made in the image of God (*imago Dei*) means that human beings reflect certain divine attributes and have a unique relationship with God. The Church Fathers teach that this image distinguishes us from the rest of creation, giving us rationality, free will, and the potential for deification (*theosis*).

## What We Share with God

1. **Rationality and Intellect**
    - *"Put on the new man who is renewed in knowledge according to the image of Him who created him."* — Colossians 3:10
    - We have the ability to reason, seek truth, and understand divine realities. Unlike animals, we are called to know God and contemplate Him.
2. **Free Will and Moral Responsibility**
    - *"You shall be holy, for I the Lord your God am holy."* — Leviticus 19:2 (LXX)
    - God is free, and so are we. We can choose between good and evil, making us morally responsible for our actions. This freedom is given so that we may choose God.
3. **Spiritual Nature and Immortality**
    - *"Then God formed man from the dust of the ground and breathed into his face the breath of life; and man became a living soul."* — Genesis 2:7 (LXX)
    - Unlike animals, humans have a rational soul that is immortal. Though the body perishes, the soul continues in eternity.
4. **Dominion and Stewardship Over Creation**
    - *"Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness; let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, over the birds of the air, and over the cattle, over all the earth."* — Genesis 1:26 (LXX)
    - God gave humanity authority over creation, not to exploit it, but to care for it as stewards.
5. **Love and Communion**
    - *"God is love, and he who abides in love abides in God, and God in him."* — 1 John 4:16
    - We are made for relationships—both with God and with each other. As the Holy Trinity is a communion of Persons, so too are we called to love and be in communion.
6. **Creativity and Participation in God’s Work**
    - *"For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works."* — Ephesians 2:10
    - Just as God creates, we have the ability to create, whether through art, music, technology, or children.
7. **Potential for Theosis (Union with God)**
    - *"That by these you may become partakers of the divine nature."* — 2 Peter 1:4
    - The ultimate goal of being made in God's image is deification (theosis)—to grow in likeness to God and become united with Him through grace.

## Distinction: Image vs. Likeness

The image (*εἰκών*) of God is something inherent—we always bear it. The likeness (*ὁμοίωσις*) is something we must grow into through a life of virtue and communion with God. The Fall distorted the likeness, but through Christ, the image can be restored and perfected.

Thus, being made in the image of God means we have rationality, freedom, love, creativity, and the capacity for holiness, all of which lead us toward union with God.
