## Jeremiah 17:9

*"The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?"*

This verse highlights the corrupt nature of the human heart and how it can lead people astray. Jeremiah warns that relying on human opinion or desires can result in deception and moral failure, as human judgment is tainted by sin.

Additionally, in **Jeremiah 7:24**, he laments how the people followed their own counsel rather than God's guidance:

## Jeremiah 7:24

*"But they did not listen or pay attention; instead, they followed the stubborn inclinations of their evil hearts. They went backward and not forward."*

This passage reinforces the theme of corruption stemming from people following their own opinions, rather than the word of God, leading to a decline in righteousness.
