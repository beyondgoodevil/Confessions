## The Jerusalem Council

- **15:6** – The apostles and elders come together to consider the matter.
- **15:7-11** – After much dispute, Peter speaks: God chose that the Gentiles should hear the gospel through him, gave them the Holy Spirit, and made no distinction between Jew and Gentile; they should not be burdened with a yoke, since all are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus.
- **15:12** – The assembly falls silent and listens to Barnabas and Paul recount the miracles God worked among the Gentiles.
- **15:13-18** – James answers, citing the prophets (the rebuilding of the tabernacle of David, so that the rest of mankind and the Gentiles may seek the Lord).
- **15:19-21** – James gives his judgment: do not trouble the Gentiles who are turning to God, but write to them to abstain from things polluted by idols, from sexual immorality, from things strangled, and from blood.

## The Jerusalem Decree

- **15:22** – The apostles and elders, with the whole church, decide to send chosen men, Judas called Barsabas and Silas, to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas.
