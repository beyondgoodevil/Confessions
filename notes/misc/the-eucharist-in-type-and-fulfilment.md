The Eucharist doesn't come out of nowhere at the Last Supper. Bread and wine from a priest-king, a lamb whose blood saves, bread from heaven, a covenant sealed in blood and then a meal. Collecting the types and then the fulfilments. KJV throughout.

## 1. Old Testament Types

### Melchizedek

**Genesis 14:18**  
"And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God."

- Priest *and* king, offering bread and wine, before there's any Levitical priesthood.
- Christ is "a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek" (Ps 110:4; Heb 7). Hebrews never mentions the bread and wine, oddly, but the Fathers all do.

### Passover Lamb

**Exodus 12:8, 13**  
"And they shall eat the flesh in that night, roast with fire, and unleavened bread... and when I see the blood, I will pass over you."

- Key point: killing the lamb isn't enough. It has to be **eaten**. And its blood saves from death.

### Manna

**Exodus 16:4, 15**  
"Behold, I will rain bread from heaven for you... This is the bread which the LORD hath given you to eat."

- Daily bread from heaven on the way to the promised land. Christ contrasts Himself with this directly in John 6.

### Blood of the Covenant, Meal on the Mountain

**Exodus 24:8, 11**  
"Behold the blood of the covenant, which the LORD hath made with you... also they saw God, and did eat and drink."

- Covenant sealed with blood, *then* they eat and drink in God's presence. The pattern is sacrifice → meal.

### Bread of the Presence

**Exodus 25:30**  
"And thou shalt set upon the table shewbread before me alway."

- Holy bread always before the LORD, eaten by the priests (Lev 24:5-9). Literally "bread of the face".

### Cup, Wisdom's Table, the Coal

**Psalm 116:13**  
"I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the LORD."

**Proverbs 9:5**  
"Come, eat of my bread, and drink of the wine which I have mingled."

**Isaiah 6:6-7**  
"Then flew one of the seraphims unto me, having a live coal in his hand, which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar: And he laid it upon my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips; and thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged."

- The coal is the one the Liturgy itself uses: the priest says "this hath touched thy lips..." after communing. The spoon is sometimes compared to the tongs.

### Pure Offering Among the Nations

**Malachi 1:11**  
"For from the rising of the sun even unto the going down of the same my name shall be great among the Gentiles; and in every place incense shall be offered unto my name, and a pure offering."

- A sacrifice offered *everywhere*, by *Gentiles*. Can't be the Temple sacrifices (one place, Israel only).
- Didache 14 and St. Justin (Dialogue with Trypho 41) both apply it to the Eucharist. That's very early evidence that Christians called the Eucharist a sacrifice.

## 2. Christ Announces It

**John 6:51**  
"I am the living bread which came down from heaven: if any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever: and the bread that I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world."

**John 6:53-56**  
"Except ye eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, ye have no life in you. Whoso eateth my flesh, and drinketh my blood, hath eternal life; and I will raise him up at the last day. For my flesh is meat indeed, and my blood is drink indeed. He that eateth my flesh, and drinketh my blood, dwelleth in me, and I in him."

- Sets Himself against the manna (6:49-50). True bread from heaven.
- Many disciples leave over this "hard saying" (6:60, 66). He lets them go. He doesn't call after them that it was a figure of speech.
- I've read that the Greek verb shifts in v. 54 to a cruder word for eating (*trogo*, chew/gnaw), which would make a symbolic reading harder. Need to check that properly.

## 3. The Institution

**Matthew 26:26-28**  
"Take, eat; this is my body... Drink ye all of it; For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins."

**Luke 22:19**  
"This do in remembrance of me."

**1 Corinthians 5:7**  
"For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us."

- It's at Passover. And "my blood of the new testament" answers Moses' "blood of the covenant" (Ex 24:8) nearly word for word.
- "Remembrance" (*anamnesis*) is sacrificial language in the LXX, a memorial before God, not just calling something to mind.

## 4. The Apostolic Church

**Luke 24:30-31, 35**  
"He took bread, and blessed it, and brake, and gave to them. And their eyes were opened, and they knew him... he was known of them in breaking of bread."

**Acts 2:42**  
"And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers."

**1 Corinthians 10:16**  
"The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?"

**1 Corinthians 11:27, 29**  
"Wherefore whosoever shall eat this bread, and drink this cup of the Lord, unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord... not discerning the Lord's body."

**Hebrews 13:10**  
"We have an altar, whereof they have no right to eat which serve the tabernacle."

- 1 Cor 11 is the strongest for the real presence, I think. You can't be "guilty of the body and blood" by mishandling a symbol. And people are getting sick and dying over it (11:30).
- Emmaus: took, blessed, broke, gave. Same four verbs as the Last Supper.

## 5. The End

**Revelation 19:9**  
"Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb."

- Every Liturgy is a foretaste of this.

## 6. Side by Side

| Type | Fulfilment |
|---|---|
| Melchizedek offers bread and wine | Christ, priest after that order, gives bread and wine as His Body and Blood |
| Passover lamb eaten, blood saves from death | Christ our Passover, flesh eaten, blood gives life |
| Manna in the wilderness | "I am the living bread which came down from heaven" |
| Blood of the covenant at Sinai | "My blood of the new testament" |
| Bread of the Presence | Christ present on the altar |
| Coal on Isaiah's lips | Communion "for the remission of sins" |
| Pure offering in every place | Eucharist offered in every nation |

Missing from this list and worth adding later: the tree of life, Elijah's bread for the journey (1 Kings 19), the feeding of the five thousand as the bridge to John 6.
