---

**Genesis 15:6** says, *"And he ==believed the LORD=="* not *"believed in the LORD."* This distinction shows that Abraham trusted what God specifically **said** to him, rather than simply holding a general belief **in** God's existence.

This underscores that Abraham's righteousness came from trusting the specific promises and covenant God made with him—not merely from a general faith. Abraham believed God's direct word about his descendants, highlighting that true faith is trust in the concrete promises and actions of God in a covenantal relationship.
