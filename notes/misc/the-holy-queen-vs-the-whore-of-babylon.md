### Revelation 12: The Holy Queen

**Key Verses:**

**Revelation 12:1-2**  
"A woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars... a man child, who was to rule all nations."

**Revelation 12:17**  
"The dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed."

**Summary:**  
The woman symbolizes Mary and the Church, representing purity and divine favor. She bears the Messiah, who will rule nations. The dragon (Satan) seeks to destroy her and her child, but God protects her, illustrating the battle between good and evil.

---

### Revelation 17: The Whore of Babylon

**Key Verses:**

**Revelation 17:1-2**  
"I will shew unto thee the judgment of the great whore that sitteth upon many waters... with whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication."

**Revelation 17:4-5**  
"The woman was arrayed in purple and scarlet, having a golden cup in her hand full of abominations... MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT."

**Summary:**  
The Whore of Babylon represents false religion and corruption, leading nations astray from true worship. She embodies immorality and faces destruction as her influence wanes, signifying the defeat of evil.

---

### Comparison: Holy Queen vs. Whore of Babylon

1. Symbolism:
    - **Holy Queen:** Holiness, divine motherhood.
    - **Whore of Babylon:** Corruption, false worship.
2. Divine Favor vs. Judgment:
    - **Holy Queen:** God's protection.
    - **Whore of Babylon:** Divine judgment.
3. Influence:
    - **Holy Queen:** Love and truth.
    - **Whore of Babylon:** Seduction and deception.
4. Ultimate Fate:
    - **Holy Queen:** Victorious through Christ.
    - **Whore of Babylon:** Faces destruction.

---

### Conclusion

These figures highlight the spiritual conflict between good and evil, with the **Holy Queen** symbolizing hope and the establishment of God's kingdom, while the **Whore of Babylon** warns of the consequences of idolatry and rebellion against God.
