***"For what have I to do to judge them also that are without? do not ye judge them that are within? But them that are without God judgeth. Therefore put away from among yourselves that wicked person."***

— 1 Corinthians 5:12-13 (KJV)

## Explanation

- **"Them that are without"** refers to those outside the Church (non-believers or those not living under the authority of Christ).
- **"God judgeth"** means that ultimate judgment over non-believers belongs to God, not to Christians.
- **"Do not ye judge them that are within?"** emphasizes that **the Church must discipline and correct its own members**, ensuring holiness within the body of Christ.
- **"Put away from among yourselves that wicked person"** is a reference to excommunication—Paul is addressing a case of grievous sin **inside** the Church (a man living in incest).

## Orthodox Interpretation

- **Judgment within the Church**: The Church has the authority to call its members to repentance and, if necessary, apply discipline (excommunication).
- **God's judgment outside the Church**: Christians are not called to pass final judgment on those outside, as **only God** knows their hearts. However, this does not mean we should not evangelize or warn against sin—it simply means that the Church’s disciplinary authority applies to its own members.
