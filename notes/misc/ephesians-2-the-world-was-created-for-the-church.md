### Relevant Passages in Ephesians 2:

1. Unity in Christ:
    - **Ephesians 2:14-16:** "For He Himself is our peace, who has made both one, and has broken down the middle wall of separation, having abolished in His flesh the enmity, that is, the law of commandments contained in ordinances, so as to create in Himself one new man from the two, thus making peace, and that He might reconcile them both to God in one body through the cross, thereby putting to death the enmity."
2. God's Purpose:
    - **Ephesians 2:19-22:** "Now, therefore, you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God, having been built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ Himself being the chief cornerstone, in whom the whole building, being fitted together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord, in whom you also are being built together for a dwelling place of God in the Spirit."

### Implications for the Church and Creation:

- **Cosmic Significance:** While Ephesians 2 does not directly state that the world was created for the Church, the implication is that the Church, as the Body of Christ, has a central role in God's plan. The Church is portrayed as the new creation, where Jews and Gentiles are united in Christ.
- **Purpose of Creation:** The broader theological understanding within Eastern Orthodox thought is that all of creation is ultimately oriented toward the glorification of God, with the Church playing a pivotal role in this process as the community that embodies and expresses God's presence and grace in the world.

### Conclusion

To summarize, while Ephesians 2 does not specifically articulate that the world was created for the Church, it underscores the Church's significance within God's redemptive plan, which implies a close relationship between the Church and creation. This understanding is supported by other biblical passages and the theological interpretations within the Eastern Orthodox tradition.
