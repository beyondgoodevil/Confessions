## Argument: The Qur'an's Confirmation of the Bible and the Immutability of God's Word

### 1. The Qur'an's Confirmation of the Bible

- **1.1. Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:46):**
    - **Arabic:**

      وَقَفَّيْنَا عَلَىٰ آثَارِهِم بِعِيسَى ابْنِ مَرْيَمَ مُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ مِنَ التَّوْرَاةِ ۖ وَآتَيْنَاهُ الْإِنجِيلَ فِيهِ هُدًى وَنُورٌ وَمُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ مِنَ التَّوْرَاةِ وَهُدًى وَمَوْعِظَةً لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ
    - **Translation:**

      "And We sent, following in their footsteps, Jesus, the son of Mary, confirming that which came before him in the Torah; and We gave him the Gospel, in which was guidance and light and confirming that which preceded it of the Torah as guidance and instruction for the righteous."
    - **Key Point:**

      This verse affirms that the Gospel (Injil) given to Jesus is a divine revelation and confirms the Torah, recognizing the Bible's authority.
- **1.2. Surah Al-Baqarah (2:285):**
    - **Arabic:**

      آمَنَ الرَّسُولُ بِمَا أُنزِلَ إِلَيْهِ مِن رَّبِّهِ وَالْمُؤْمِنُونَ ۚ كُلٌّ آمَنَ بِاللَّهِ وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ
    - **Translation:**

      "The Messenger has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, and so have the believers. All of them have believed in Allah and His angels and His books and His messengers..."
    - **Key Point:**

      Muhammad and the believers accepted all divine scriptures, including those that preceded the Qur'an.

### 2. The Immutability of God's Word

- **2.1. Surah Al-An'am (6:115):**
    - **Arabic:**

      وَتَمَّتْ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ صِدْقًا وَعَدْلًا ۚ لَّا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمَاتِهِ ۚ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
    - **Translation:**

      "And the word of your Lord has been fulfilled in truth and in justice. None can alter His words, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing."
    - **Key Point:**

      No one can change or corrupt God's words, affirming the preservation of all divine revelations.
- **2.2. Surah Al-Kahf (18:27):**
    - **Arabic:**

      وَاتْلُ مَا أُوحِيَ إِلَيْكَ مِن كِتَابِ رَبِّكَ ۖ لَا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمَاتِهِ وَلَن تَجِدَ مِن دُونِهِ مُلْتَحَدًا
    - **Translation:**

      "And recite what has been revealed to you of the Book of your Lord. There is no changer of His words, and never will you find in other than Him a refuge."
    - **Key Point:**

      God's words cannot be altered, underscoring the divine authority and preservation of previous scriptures as well as the Qur'an.

### 3. The Historical Context of the Bible

- **3.1. The Codex Sinaiticus:**
    - **Description:**

      The oldest preserved version of the Bible that aligns with the Orthodox Christian canon, dating to the 4th century (330–360 AD).
    - **Significance:**

      This manuscript existed several centuries before the Prophet Muhammad and includes texts that are consistent with the Christian Bible.

### 4. Theological Perspective on God's Eternity

- **4.1. Sunni Belief:**
    - **Key Doctrine:**

      God is eternal and uncreated, while creation is finite in origin.
    - **Distinction:**

      God existed before creation, which came into being by His will.

---

### Summary

1. The Qur'an confirms the divine origin and authority of earlier scriptures, including the Torah and Gospel.
2. The Qur'an asserts that God's words are immutable and cannot be changed.
3. Historical evidence shows that the Bible's canonical texts predate Islam and were preserved.
4. Sunni theology maintains God's eternal nature, distinguishing Him from creation.
