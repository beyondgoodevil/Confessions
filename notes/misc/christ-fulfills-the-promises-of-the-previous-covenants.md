title: Christ Fulfills the Promises of the Previous Covenants
tags: [theology, covenant, scripture]
summary: The promise of each of the six covenants and its fulfilment in Christ.
---

1. **Adamic Covenant:**
    - **Promise:** Victory over sin and death (Genesis 3:15).
    - **Fulfillment:** Christ, as the "second Adam," conquers sin and death through His obedience and resurrection, restoring humanity (1 Corinthians 15:45).
2. **Noahic Covenant:**
    - **Promise:** Preservation of life and a new beginning (Genesis 9:8-17).
    - **Fulfillment:** Christ's resurrection inaugurates a new creation, offering salvation and renewal to all believers (2 Corinthians 5:17).
3. **Abrahamic Covenant:**
    - **Promise:** Blessing to all nations (Genesis 12:1-3).
    - **Fulfillment:** Through Christ, the blessings promised to Abraham are realized for all nations, uniting believers into one body (Galatians 3:14).
4. **Mosaic Covenant:**
    - **Promise:** A covenant of law and relationship with God (Exodus 19-24).
    - **Fulfillment:** Christ fulfills the Law through His life, teachings, and sacrificial death, providing a new way to relate to God through grace (Romans 10:4).
5. **Davidic Covenant:**
    - **Promise:** An everlasting kingdom through David's lineage (2 Samuel 7:12-16).
    - **Fulfillment:** Christ, as the descendant of David, reigns eternally as King, establishing His Kingdom (Luke 1:32-33).
6. **New Covenant:**
    - **Promise:** Forgiveness and a renewed relationship with God (Jeremiah 31:31-34).
    - **Fulfillment:** In His blood, Christ establishes the New Covenant, offering redemption and the indwelling of the Holy Spirit (Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15).
