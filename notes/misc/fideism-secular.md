---
title: Fideism (secular)
tags: [theology, epistemology]
summary: Fideism outside a religious context — belief held without evidence or argument.
---

Fideism, outside of a religious context, refers to an approach where belief or trust in a particular idea, principle, or system is maintained without requiring evidence, logical justification, or rational argumentation. It prioritizes intuition, emotion, or subjective experience over empirical proof or reasoned analysis. This can manifest in various ways, such as accepting political ideologies, philosophical stances, or scientific theories based on faith in authority or tradition rather than critical examination.
