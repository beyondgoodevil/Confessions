## Canons of the Council of Constantinople

1. **Canon 1:** Confirms the Nicene Creed and the faith of the 318 Fathers at Nicaea, asserting that it must be adhered to.
2. **Canon 2:** Establishes the authority of the Bishop of Constantinople, granting him the same privileges as the Bishop of Rome.
3. **Canon 3:** Declares that all bishops must adhere to the teachings and practices established by the council.
4. **Canon 4:** States that bishops should not be appointed or ordained without the consent of the local bishop.
5. **Canon 5:** Forbids bishops from taking part in the proceedings of other bishops without their consent.
6. **Canon 6:** Addresses the relationship between the bishop and the clergy, mandating that the clergy should obey their bishop.
7. **Canon 7:** Regulates the election of bishops, ensuring that it is conducted with due process.
8. **Canon 8:** Discusses the issue of clerical celibacy, stating that married clergy may not marry again after ordination.
9. **Canon 9:** Declares that the clergy who have fallen into sin must perform penance before being readmitted to their positions.
10. **Canon 10:** Addresses the issue of those who have lapsed in faith, emphasizing the need for penance before readmission.
11. **Canon 11:** States that those who are expelled from the Church should remain in a state of penance until they demonstrate true repentance.
12. **Canon 12:** Prohibits those who have served in the military from being admitted to the clergy.
13. **Canon 13:** Establishes rules regarding the conduct of clergy, particularly in relation to their behavior and obligations.
14. **Canon 14:** Addresses the treatment of heretics, allowing for their return to the Church under specific conditions.
15. **Canon 15:** Outlines the importance of maintaining the unity of the Church and avoiding schisms.
16. **Canon 16:** Forbids the transfer of bishops without legitimate cause, ensuring stability within the Church.
17. **Canon 17:** Prohibits clergy from engaging in usury (charging interest on loans).
18. **Canon 18:** Restricts deacons from presiding over the Eucharist.
19. **Canon 19:** Addresses the issue of the return of heretical groups to the Church, requiring rebaptism.
20. **Canon 20:** Prohibits kneeling during prayers on Sundays and from Easter to Pentecost.
21. **Canon 21:** Declares that clerics who have lapsed should not be promoted to higher office without demonstrating repentance.
22. **Canon 22:** Addresses the role of women in the Church, establishing guidelines for their participation.
23. **Canon 23:** Forbids self-castration as a form of piety or asceticism among clergy.
24. **Canon 24:** Discusses the treatment of catechumens and their admission to the Church.
25. **Canon 25:** Addresses the role of laity in church matters, ensuring their participation is respected.
26. **Canon 26:** Declares that heretics may not be promoted to clerical positions.
27. **Canon 27:** Prohibits the imposition of a single tradition over another in matters of practice.
28. **Canon 28:** Reaffirms the privileges of the Bishop of Constantinople and the role of the Patriarch.
