Paul refers to assigning Timothy to lead the church in Ephesus in **1 Timothy 1:3**.

## 1 Timothy 1:3

Paul reminds Timothy that, when he left for Macedonia, he urged him to remain in Ephesus so that he might charge certain people to teach no other doctrine. *(Paraphrased; the image quotes the copyrighted New King James Version.)*

## Context and Significance

- **Leadership Role**: Paul is instructing Timothy to stay in Ephesus to provide pastoral oversight, particularly to combat false teaching.
- **Timothy's Mission**: His role involves maintaining doctrinal purity, encouraging sound teaching, and guiding the church community.
- **Apostolic Delegation**: This reflects the broader apostolic practice of appointing trusted leaders (bishops, presbyters) to oversee specific local churches.

This passage highlights Paul's mentorship and Timothy's important role in early Church governance.
