## The Beloved Disciple and His Book

- **21:20-21** – Peter turns and sees the disciple whom Jesus loved following, and asks, "But Lord, what about this man?"
- **21:22** – Jesus answers that if He wills that he remain till He comes, what is that to Peter? "You follow Me."
- **21:23** – A saying spreads among the brethren that this disciple would not die, though Jesus did not say that.
- **21:24** – This is the disciple who testifies of these things and wrote them, and his testimony is true.
- **21:25** (highlighted) – ==There are also many other things that Jesus did; if they were written one by one, the world itself could not contain the books.==
