## Laws Still Kept in the New Covenant

1. Moral Law:

   **Fulfillment:** The moral commandments, particularly the Ten Commandments, are seen as eternal principles that reflect God's character. They are upheld in the New Covenant as expressions of love and righteousness (Matthew 5:17-19).

2. Love Commandments:

   **Fulfillment:** Christ summarizes the law in the commandments to love God and love one's neighbor (Matthew 22:37-40). This principle of love transcends specific legalistic observances and embodies the essence of the moral law.

3. Ethical Teachings:

   **Fulfillment:** Many ethical teachings found in the Mosaic Law, such as those related to justice, honesty, and mercy, are emphasized by Christ and remain relevant for believers (James 1:27).

4. Sabbath Rest:

   **Fulfillment:** While the observance of the Sabbath is transformed in Christ, the principle of rest and worship is still upheld in the New Covenant. The Orthodox Church celebrates Sunday as the Lord's Day, commemorating Christ's resurrection.

## Laws Fulfilled in the New Covenant

1. Ceremonial Laws:

   **Fulfillment:** The rituals and sacrifices of the Mosaic Law (e.g., animal sacrifices, dietary laws) are fulfilled in Christ, who is the ultimate sacrifice for sin (Hebrews 10:1-4). Believers are no longer bound by these ceremonial observances.

2. Cultic Laws:

   **Fulfillment:** The laws pertaining to the priesthood and temple worship are fulfilled in Christ as the High Priest and in the establishment of the Church as the new temple (Hebrews 4:14-16).

3. Dietary Laws:

   **Fulfillment:** The dietary restrictions of the Mosaic Law are also fulfilled. In Acts 10:9-16, Peter receives a vision that indicates the removal of these dietary restrictions, affirming that all foods are clean.
