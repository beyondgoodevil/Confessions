## Luke 1:26-38 (ESV)

In this passage, the angel Gabriel announces to Mary that she will conceive and bear a son, who will be the Son of God.

## Luke 1:38 (ESV)

**"And Mary said, 'Behold, I am the servant of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word.' And the angel departed from her."**

## Key Elements

1. **Gabriel's Announcement:** In **Luke 1:26-37**, Gabriel tells Mary that she will miraculously conceive Jesus, the **Son of the Most High**, by the power of the Holy Spirit.
2. **Mary's Response:** In **Luke 1:38**, Mary gives her consent by humbly accepting God's plan, saying, **"Behold, I am the servant of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word."**
    - This statement shows Mary's free and willing acceptance of her role in God's plan of salvation.
    - By calling herself the **"servant"** (or "handmaid"), Mary acknowledges her submission to God's will.

## Theological Significance

Mary's consent in this moment is seen as crucial in the divine plan of salvation. Her **"yes"** (often referred to as her **Fiat**, Latin for "Let it be") is a model of obedience and faith, marking the moment when the **Incarnation** begins, as the eternal Word of God takes on human flesh in her womb. This act of voluntary participation contrasts with Eve's disobedience and is often seen in Christian theology as Mary's role in reversing the effects of the Fall.
