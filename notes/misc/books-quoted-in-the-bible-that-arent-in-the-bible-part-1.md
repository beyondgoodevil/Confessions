1. **The Book of the Wars of the Lord**
    - *Numbers 21:14*
2. "Therefore it is said in the Book of the Wars of the Lord..."  
   This appears to be a collection of war-related records or songs used by the Israelites.
3. **The Book of Jasher (or Jashar)**
    - *Joshua 10:13*, *2 Samuel 1:18*
4. "Is it not written in the Book of Jashar?"  
   It seems to have contained poetic accounts of heroic deeds.
5. **The Book of the Acts of Solomon**
    - *1 Kings 11:41*
6. "Now the rest of the acts of Solomon... are they not written in the book of the acts of Solomon?"  
   A royal chronicle possibly containing detailed accounts of his reign.
7. **The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel**
    - *1 Kings 14:19* and many other verses throughout Kings  
      Frequently cited as a historical record of Israel's kings, separate from the canonical Chronicles.
8. **The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah**
    - *1 Kings 14:29*, *2 Chronicles 16:11*  
      Similar to the above, but focused on Judah's kings.
9. **The Book of Nathan the Prophet**
    - *1 Chronicles 29:29*, *2 Chronicles 9:29*  
      Mentioned as a prophetic record of David's reign.
10. **The Prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite**
    - *2 Chronicles 9:29*  
      Cited as a prophetic source detailing events during Solomon's reign.
11. **The Visions of Iddo the Seer**
    - *2 Chronicles 9:29*, *2 Chronicles 12:15*  
      Another prophetic book no longer extant.
12. **The Book of Shemaiah the Prophet**
    - *2 Chronicles 12:15*  
      Used for the history of Rehoboam’s reign.
13. **The Book of Jehu the Son of Hanani**
    - *2 Chronicles 20:34*  
      Chronicles events in the reign of Jehoshaphat.
14. **The Sayings of the Seers**
    - *2 Chronicles 33:19*  
      Mentioned in relation to the acts of King Manasseh.
