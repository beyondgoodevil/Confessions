---
title: The Fall of humanity and its consequences
tags: [theology, scripture]
summary: Eight passages on the Fall, sin, death and separation from God.
---

1. **Genesis 3:6 – The Fall**

   *"So when the woman saw that the tree was good for food, that it was pleasant to the eyes, and a tree desirable to make one wise, she took of its fruit and ate. She also gave to her husband with her, and he ate."*

   (The moment Adam and Eve disobeyed God and sinned.)

2. **Genesis 3:16-19 – Consequences for Humanity**

   *"To the woman He said: 'I will greatly multiply your sorrow and your conception; in pain you shall bring forth children; your desire shall be for your husband, and he shall rule over you.' Then to Adam He said, '...Cursed is the ground for your sake; in toil you shall eat of it all the days of your life. ... For dust you are, and to dust you shall return.'"*

   (Consequences: pain, toil, and death.)

3. **Romans 5:12 – Sin Entering the World**

   *"Therefore, just as through one man sin entered the world, and death through sin, and thus death spread to all men, because all sinned."*

   (The universality of sin and death due to Adam's transgression.)

4. **Romans 3:23 – All Have Sinned**

   *"For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."*

   (Every human being is affected by the Fall.)

5. **1 Corinthians 15:21-22 – Death and Resurrection**

   *"For since by man came death, by Man also came the resurrection of the dead. For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ all shall be made alive."*

   (Adam brought death, but Christ brings life.)

6. **Psalm 51:5 – Original Sin**

   *"Behold, I was brought forth in iniquity, and in sin my mother conceived me."*

   (The sinful nature is inherited.)

7. **Isaiah 59:2 – Separation from God**

   *"But your iniquities have separated you from your God; and your sins have hidden His face from you, so that He will not hear."*

   (The Fall causes a separation between humanity and God.)

8. **Romans 6:23 – Wages of Sin**

   *"For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."*

   (The ultimate consequence of sin is death, but redemption is available through Christ.)
