Start with baptism itself, then what makes a sacrament valid, then the Donatist dispute.

## Baptism

Scripture and practice.

1. [[Infant Baptism Verses and Context]] — Covenant, household baptisms and early Church practice as the basis for baptising infants.
2. [[Criteria for Valid Baptism in the Orthodox Church]] — Form, matter, intention and canonical standing, and how the Church judges and receives baptisms.
3. [[1 Peter 3:20-21: Baptism Is Like the Ark]] — 1 Peter 3:20-21, with "baptism" highlighted as the antitype of the ark.
4. [[Acts 19:1–6: Paul Episcopate]] — Acts 19:1-6 (KJV), where Paul completes the initiation of John's disciples by baptism and the laying on of hands.

## The Eucharist

Type, institution and apostolic practice.

1. [[The Eucharist in Type and Fulfilment]] — OT types of the Eucharist (Melchizedek, Passover, manna, covenant blood, shewbread, Isaiah's coal, Malachi's pure offering) and their fulfilment in John 6, the Last Supper and the early Church.
2. [[Key Teachings of the Council of Ephesus]] — Five teachings of Ephesus and their bearing on the Eucharist.

## Validity

East and West on what a sacrament requires.

1. [[3 Necessary Requirements of a Valid Sacrament (RC)]] — Roman Catholic canon law — proper matter, proper form, and proper minister with intent.
2. [[Orthodox Teaching About the Sacraments Being Valid Only Within the Canonical Boundaries of the Church]] — Four points on why the sacraments are efficacious only within the Church.
3. [[Economia]] — The Orthodox principle of applying Church rules flexibly for the sake of salvation, contrasted with akrivia.
4. [[Legalistic View on Baptism: Article, Roman Catholic Church]] — Summary of a 2022 NPR report on Catholic baptisms ruled invalid over a single word.

## Donatism

Does the minister's worthiness matter?

1. [[Donatism]] — The 4th-century North African movement that tied sacramental validity to the purity of the clergy.
2. [[Augustinian View on Baptism]] — Augustine on why baptism's validity does not depend on the minister, and why it profits only within the Church.
3. [[EO View on Donatism]] — Five reasons Orthodoxy is not Donatist, and how the East responded to Donatism.
