Start with the Gospel accounts, then the Old Testament types, then her titles and the history.

## In the Gospels

The Annunciation and the Magnificat.

1. [[Gabriel's Announcement to Mary]] — Five passages from Luke 1-2 (KJV) on the Annunciation and the birth of Jesus.
2. [[Mary's Consent to the Angel Gabriel]] — Luke 1:38 and the significance of Mary's "let it be".
3. [[The Prayer of Mary Found in the Gospel of Luke]] — The Magnificat (Luke 1:46-55, KJV) with a short summary.
4. [[The Virgin Birth of Mary: Verses]] — Isaiah 7:14, Matthew 1:18-25 and Luke 1:26-38 on the Virgin Birth.

## Old Testament Types and Promises

What foreshadowed her.

1. [[Types of Mary in the OT]] — Seven Old Testament types of Mary, from Eve to Hannah.
2. [[Virgin Births in the OT]] — Sarah, Rachel, Hannah and Elizabeth — barren women granted children, foreshadowing the virgin birth.
3. [[Sarah and Hannah Experienced Miraculous Births: Verses]] — Genesis 21:1-3 and 1 Samuel 1:19-20 on the births of Isaac and Samuel.
4. [[Mary and the Promises Made to Women That Foreshadow the Miraculous Birth of Jesus]] — Old Testament promises to Eve, Sarah, Rachel and Hannah, Isaiah's prophecy, and their fulfilment in Mary.
5. [[Symbolism of Aaron’s Rod in Relation to Christ and Mary]] — Aaron's budding rod and the rod of Jesse as types of Christ and of Mary.
6. [[Mary as the Ark of the Covenant and as the New Heavenly Jerusalem, Symbolizing the Ultimate Image of the Church: Verses]] — Verses for Mary as the Ark, as the New Jerusalem, and as the image of the Church.

## The Woman and the Queen

Genesis 3:15 to Revelation 12.

1. [[Mary Referred to as The Woman in the Bible]] — Six passages where Mary is called or identified as "the woman".
2. [[Revelation 12, Genesis 3:15: Mary Prophecy]] — Three parallels between the woman of Genesis 3:15 and the woman of Revelation 12.
3. [[Mary the Most Pure Queen of Heaven Next to Christ]] — Five aspects of Mary's place — purity, queenship, nearness to Christ, intercession and the Incarnation.
4. [[The Holy Queen vs The Whore of Babylon]] — The woman of Revelation 12 contrasted with the woman of Revelation 17.
5. [[The Messiah and His Bride]] — Marriage imagery for God and His people in both Testaments, and Mary as its archetype.

The title Theotokos and later disputes.

2. [[Saint Jerome Defends Virginity of the Theotokos]] — Four points on Saint Jerome's defence of Mary as Theotokos.
