Start with the distinction, then the light of Tabor, then theosis and prayer.

## The Distinction

God unknowable in essence, truly known in His energies.

1. [[Essence-Energies Distinction]] — God's essence and His uncreated energies, created energies of angels and demons, and the Fathers who taught the distinction.
2. [[God's Uncreated Energies]] — Ten of God's uncreated energies, each in a line.
3. [[Saint Gregory of Nyssa: Essence Energies]] — Gregory's doctrine of epektasis — the soul's endless ascent toward God through His energies.
4. [[Implications of No Essence-Energies Distinction]] — Why identifying God's essence with His energies would make His actions necessary rather than free.
5. [[Jesus Walking on Water: Eternal Act, Essence-Energy Distinction]] — Without an essence-energies distinction, a miracle such as walking on water would be an eternal act rather than a historical event.

## The Uncreated Light

Glory seen on Tabor and on the Damascus road.

1. [[The Transfiguration of Jesus on Mount Tabor]] — The Gospel accounts of the Transfiguration and its meaning as a revelation of the divine energies.
2. [[Matthew 17: Jesus Transfigured on the Mount]] — Matthew 17:1-13, the Transfiguration, with "light" highlighted in verse 2.
3. [[1 Timothy 6:16: God Dwells in Unapproachable Light, aka His Uncreated Glory]] — 1 Timothy 6:16 — God alone has immortality and dwells in unapproachable light.
4. [[Acts 9:1-9: Paul (Saul) Sees Christ as a Light From Heaven]] — Acts 9:1-9 (NKJV) — the Damascus road.
5. [[2 Corinthians 3:18: We Move From Glory to Glory]] — 2 Corinthians 3:18 — beholding the glory of the Lord, we are transformed from glory to glory.

## Theosis

Partaking of the divine nature.

1. [[2 Peter 1:3-4: Theosis]] — 2 Peter 1:3-4 on divine power and becoming partakers of the divine nature.
2. [[Being Made in the Image of God]] — Seven things man shares with God, and the distinction between image and likeness.
3. [[The Logoi of Creation in St. Maximus]] — St. Maximus on the logoi. Every creature has its logos in the one Logos; logos vs tropos; being, well-being, eternal well-being; the reply to Origenism; natural contemplation.
4. [[Church Fathers That Taught the Doctrine of the Deification of Human Nature Through the Incarnation]] — Seven Fathers, from Irenaeus to Photios, on deification through the Incarnation.
5. [[Nous]] — The nous as the eye of the soul in Orthodox Christianity.

## Practice

How the tradition is lived.

1. [[The Jesus Prayer and Hesychasm]] — The prayer and where it comes from in Scripture, the hesychast line from the Desert Fathers to Palamas and the Philokalia, terms, practice and the usual warnings.
2. [[Mysticism Does Not Contradict Evidentialism or Evidence]] — Eastern Orthodox mysticism transcends reason and evidence without rejecting them.
