Start with the covenants, then Abraham, then types, then prophecy and the new Israel.

## The Covenants

From Adam to the New Covenant.

1. [[List of All Covenants]] — The six biblical covenants from Adam to the New Covenant in Christ.
2. [[Christ Fulfills the Promises of the Previous Covenants]] — The promise of each of the six covenants and its fulfilment in Christ.
3. [[The Relationship Between the Old Covenant and the New Covenant]] — Typology in Galatians and Hebrews linking the Old Covenant to its fulfilment in Christ.
4. [[Laws From the Mosaic Covenant Are Still Kept and Fulfilled in the New Covenant]] — Which Mosaic laws are still kept and which are fulfilled in Christ.
5. [[Jesus Established the New Covenant]] — Luke 22:20, Matthew 26:28 and Hebrews 9:15 on the New Covenant in Christ's blood.
6. [[Believers Inherit the Promises of God Only Through Their Union With Christ]] — Five Pauline verses on being "in Christ" as the way believers inherit God's promises.

## Abraham

Faith, promise and seed.

1. [[Abraham's Journey of Faith and His Covenant Relationship With God]] — Four altars Abraham built, at Shechem, Bethel, Hebron and Moriah.
2. [[Genesis 12:1-4: Abraham Already Believed in God]] — Abraham's obedience in Genesis 12 shows faith that predates Genesis 15:6.
3. [[Genesis 15:6: Abraham Believed the Lord, Not in the Lord]] — Abraham trusted what God specifically said to him, not merely that God exists.
4. [[Genesis 15:6: Abraham Believed the Lord, Not in the Lord + Romans, Paul]] — Romans 4:3 alongside Genesis 15:6 — Abraham believed God's specific word.
5. [[Jesus Fulfills the Covenant Promises Made to Abraham]] — Four passages from Galatians on Christ as the fulfilment of the promises to Abraham.
6. [[Galatians Regarding the Descent or Lineage of Christ]] — Three verses from Galatians on Christ as Abraham's seed, born of a woman.

## Types

Persons and things that prefigure Christ and the Church.

1. [[Types of Christ in the OT]] — Ten Old Testament types of Christ, from Adam to the manna from heaven.
2. [[Types of Church in the OT]] — Nine Old Testament types of the Church, from Eve to the Tabernacle.
3. [[Adam as a Type of Christ and Eve as a Type of the Church]] — Three ways Adam prefigures Christ and four ways Eve prefigures the Church.
4. [[Noah Is a Type of Christ]] — Ten parallels between Noah and Christ.
5. [[The Melchizedekian Priesthood]] — Psalm 110:4 and Hebrews 7 on Christ's eternal priesthood after the order of Melchizedek.
6. [[The Eucharist in Type and Fulfilment]] — Old Testament types of the Eucharist and their fulfilment in John 6, the Last Supper and the apostolic Church.
7. [[1 Peter 3:20-21: Baptism Is Like the Ark]] — 1 Peter 3:20-21, with "baptism" highlighted as the antitype of the ark.
8. [[1 Peter 3:20-21: The Events of Noah's Flood Were a Type of Baptism]] — Peter presents the flood as a type of baptism — water, salvation through water, judgment and cleansing.

## Prophecy Fulfilled

What the prophets said of the Messiah.

1. [[Jesus Fulfills Prophecies]] — Fifteen Old Testament prophecies and their fulfilment in Christ, from the virgin birth to the ascension.
2. [[Jesus Fulfills Prophecies 1]] — Prophecies 1 to 6, from the virgin birth to healing and miracles.
3. [[Jesus Fulfills Prophecies 2]] — Prophecies 7 to 12, from the triumphal entry to no broken bones.
4. [[Jesus Fulfills Prophecies 3]] — Prophecies 13 to 15 — burial, resurrection and ascension.
5. [[King David Prophesied the Incarnation of the Messiah]] — Five Psalms read as prophecies of the Messiah's sonship, resurrection, suffering, kingship and exaltation.
6. [[Psalms and Isaiah Speak About Teaching of the Nations]] — Six passages from the Psalms and Isaiah on the Messiah teaching all nations.
7. [[The Coming of the Messiah: All Nations Will Be Drawn to Him]] — Prophecies that all nations will flow to the Lord, from Isaiah, Micah, Zechariah and Acts.
8. [[Luke 21:20-25]] — Luke 21:20-24, Christ's prophecy of the destruction of Jerusalem.

## Israel and the Church

The people of God, old and new.

1. [[The Twelve Tribes of Israel]] — The twelve tribes descended from Jacob's sons and grandsons, with a line on each.
2. [[The First Followers of Christ Were Jews]] — Scripture showing that Jesus' ministry, disciples and the early Church were Jewish first.
3. [[Galatians 6:15-16: The Church Is the Israel of God]] — Galatians 6:15-16 read as identifying the Church with the Israel of God.
