## Overviews

The pattern across the Old Testament.

1. [[Theophanies]] — Fifteen key theophanies in the Old Testament.
2. [[Jesus in the OT as Theophany]] — Five Old Testament theophanies read as appearances of the pre-incarnate Christ.
3. [[The Angel of the Lord]] — Ten Old Testament appearances of the Angel of the Lord read as the pre-incarnate Christ.
4. [[The Trinity Hinted in the Old Testament]] — OT verses grouped by theme (plural speech, Word and Spirit, two called LORD, the Son, Wisdom, threefold patterns) that make sense in light of the NT.

## The Passages

Each appearance in turn.

1. [[Genesis 32:24-30: Jacob Wrestled and Saw God]] — Genesis 32:24-30, where Jacob wrestles a Man and says he has seen God face to face.
2. [[Bible: Exodus 3, I AM WHO I AM, Theophany]] — Exodus 3:1-22, the burning bush and the revelation of the divine name.
3. [[Joshua 5:13-15: Joshua Met the Lord]] — Joshua 5:13-15, where Joshua worships the Commander of the army of the Lord.
4. [[Judges 13:2-22: Samson's Parents Have Seen God]] — Judges 13:2-22, where the Angel of the Lord appears to Manoah and his wife and they say they have seen God.
5. [[Isaiah 6:1-5: I Saw the Lord]] — Isaiah 6:1-5, Isaiah's vision of the Lord enthroned, with "I saw the Lord" highlighted.
