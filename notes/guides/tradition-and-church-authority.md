## Foundations

What Orthodoxy rests on.

1. [[The Basis for the Eastern Orthodox (EO) Belief]] — The seven elements of Holy Tradition on which Orthodox belief rests.
2. [[The Word Orthodoxy Meaning]] — Orthodoxy" means "correct belief", from the Greek ortho and doxa.
3. [[EO Continuity With OT]] — How the Orthodox Church sees its worship, priesthood and covenants as continuous with the Old Testament.

## Oral Tradition in Scripture

Passages showing the faith was handed on by word as well as in writing.

1. [[Overview of Where Jesus Refers to Oral Tradition]] — Six Gospel passages in which Jesus refers to or relies on oral tradition.
2. [[Paul Commands to Keep the Oral Traditions as Well as the Written Ones]] — 2 Thessalonians 2:15 and 1 Corinthians 11:2 on holding to traditions delivered by word and by letter.
3. [[Paul Either Had Access to Additional Oral Traditions of Jesus' Teachings or Through the Inspiration of the Holy Spirit]] — Five sayings Paul attributes to the Lord that are not recorded in the Gospels.
4. [[2 Thessalonians 2:15: Stand Fast and Hold the Tradition Which You Were Taught by Word or Epistle]] — 2 Thessalonians 2:15 — hold the traditions taught by word or by letter.
5. [[2 Thessalonians 2: Tradition and Holy Spirit]] — 2 Thessalonians 2:13-17, with "traditions which you were taught" highlighted.
6. [[1 Thessalonians 2:13: Paul Teaches Oral Tradition]] — 1 Thessalonians 2:13 — the preached word was received as the word of God.
7. [[1 Thessalonians 2:13: The Word of God Which You Heard From Us, You Welcomed It Not as the Word of Men, but as It Is in Truth, the Word of God]] — 1 Thessalonians 2:13, with the spoken word received as the word of God highlighted.
8. [[2 Chronicles 29: Oral Tradition, Because Hezekiah Had No Written Record About How David Had Done Something]] — 2 Chronicles 29:1-19 — Hezekiah did according to all that David had done.
9. [[John 20:30: Not Everything Jesus Did Has Been Written Down]] — John 20:24-31, with "which are not written in this book" highlighted.
10. [[John 21:20-25: And There Are Also Many Other Things That Jesus Did, Which If They Were Written One by One, I Suppose That Even the World Itself Could Not Contain the Books That Would Be Written]] — John 21:20-25, ending with the statement that not everything Jesus did was written down.
11. [[Acts 20:7-9: Paul Gave a Long Sermon, but the Details of What He Said Are Not Written Down in Scripture]] — Acts 20:7-9, where Paul preaches until midnight and none of the sermon is recorded.

## Apostolic Succession

Authority handed from the Apostles to those they appointed.

1. [[2 Timothy 2: Paul Teaches Apostolic Succession]] — 2 Timothy 2:1-13, with verse 2 on handing the teaching on to faithful men highlighted.
2. [[1 Timothy 4:14: Apostolic Succession by Paul, Laying on of Hands]] — 1 Timothy 4:14 (NKJV).
3. [[1 Timothy 1:3: Paul Puts Timothy in Charge in Ephesus]] — 1 Timothy 1:3 and what it shows about apostolic delegation.
4. [[Acts 19:1–6: Paul Episcopate]] — Acts 19:1-6 (KJV), where Paul completes the initiation of John's disciples by baptism and the laying on of hands.
5. [[Acts 18: Ministry of Apollos]] — Acts 18:24-28, where Apollos, knowing only John's baptism, is taught the way of God more accurately.
6. [[Acts: Paul Instructs]] — Seven episodes in Acts where converts are taught, baptised and brought into the fullness of the gospel.

## Authority and Government

Who may teach, bind, loose and judge.

1. [[Matthew 15, 16: Office of the Keys]] — Matthew 16:19 and 18:18 on the authority to bind and loose.
2. [[Apostles Have the Authority to Forgive Sins: Book of John]] — John 20:22-23 as the basis for the apostles' authority to forgive sins and for confession.
3. [[Authority in Mark 16]] — The signs promised to believers in Mark 16 do not give every believer the same authority, as Acts shows.
4. [[Acts 15:6-22: Church Government]] — The Jerusalem Council in Acts 15:6-22 as a model of conciliar Church government.
5. [[1 Corinthians 5:12-13: God Judges, Not We]] — 1 Corinthians 5:12-13 (KJV) on judging those within the Church and leaving those outside to God.
6. [[Acts 20:29-30: Paul Speaks About Heretical Teachings]] — Acts 20:29-30 (KJV) — Paul's warning of wolves and of false teachers arising from within.
7. [[Jeremiah 17:9 + 7:24: Corruption of Faith Due to Human Opinion]] — Jeremiah on the deceitful heart and on people following their own counsel.

## The One Church

The Church founded by Christ and guided by the Spirit.

1. [[Matthew 16:18: Jesus Says the Gates of Hell Will Not Prevail Against the Church]] — Matthew 16:18, with "My church" highlighted.
2. [[Christ Set Up His Church at Pentecost, and the Church Is Referred to as His]] — Verses on the Church's establishment at Pentecost and on the Church as the Body of Christ.
3. [[Jeremiah, Acts 2: The Beginning of the Church, Where God Dwells With His People]] — Jeremiah 31:31-33 and Acts 2:1-4 — the promised new covenant fulfilled at Pentecost.
4. [[Jesus Promises That the Holy Spirit Will Guide the Church]] — Six passages from John and Acts on the promise and coming of the Holy Spirit.
5. [[Ephesians 2: The World Was Created for the Church]] — Ephesians 2:14-22 and what it implies about the Church's place in creation.
6. [[Branch Theory Refuted From the Bible]] — Gospel and Acts passages showing that acting in Christ's name is not the same as belonging to His one Church.
