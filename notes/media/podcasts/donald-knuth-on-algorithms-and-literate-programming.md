## Themes

- **Literate programming.** Knuth's view that a program should be written as an essay for people, with the code woven into the explanation. The compiler is the second audience, not the first.
- **Why TeX exists.** He wrote TeX because he was unhappy with how the typesetting of his books had degraded, and expected it to take a few months. It took years.
- **Taking your time.** *The Art of Computer Programming* is still in progress after six decades, which he treats as normal for work meant to last.

## Connections

Knuth's patience is the opposite end of the spectrum from the urgency in [[You and your research]]; both are about choosing problems worth years of your life.
