## The core question

Hamming asked colleagues at lunch: *what are the important problems in your field, and why aren't you working on them?* Most had no good answer. The talk is his attempt to explain the difference between people who did great work and people who didn't.

## Points I want to keep

- **Work on important problems** — ones where you have an *attack*, not just ones that matter. "Important" means both.
- **Keep the door open.** People who worked with their office door open were interrupted more but ended up working on better problems.
- **Turn defects into assets.** Constraints often force the more general solution.
- **Invest in knowledge like compound interest.** A little more effort per day adds up over a career.
- **Sell the work.** Presenting results well is part of doing them.
