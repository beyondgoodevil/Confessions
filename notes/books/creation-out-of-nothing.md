Against two challenges: biblical scholars who say *ex nihilo* isn't in the Bible (Genesis describes ordering pre-existing chaos), and process theologians and Mormons who say matter is eternal. Copan does the Bible and history, Craig the philosophy and science.

## 1. The Old Testament

**Genesis 1:1-2**
- "**In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.** And the earth was **without form, and void**; and darkness was upon the face of the deep."
- Two readings:
    - **independent clause** (traditional): v.1 is the absolute beginning; v.2 describes the unformed state God made.
    - **dependent clause** ("When God began to create... the earth was formless"): v.2 describes something already there. Copan argues for the traditional reading (grammar, the Septuagint, and the pattern "heaven and earth" = everything).
- ***Bara*** ("create"): in the OT, God alone is ever its subject, and it never mentions material. Not proof alone, but suggestive.
- **"Heaven and earth"** is a merism: everything.

**Elsewhere**
- Ps 33:6, 9: "**By the word of the LORD were the heavens made**... For he spake, and it was done."
- Ps 90:2: "Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even **from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God**." God before the world.
- Prov 8:22-26: Wisdom was there "**before** the earth was... when there were no depths".
- Isa 44:24: "I am the LORD that **maketh all things**; that stretcheth forth the heavens **alone**."
- Isa 45:7: "I form the light, and create darkness."

## 2. The New Testament

- John 1:3: "**All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.**"
- Rom 4:17: "God, who quickeneth the dead, and **calleth those things which be not as though they were**."
- Heb 11:3: "the worlds were framed by the word of God, so that **things which are seen were not made of things which do appear**."
- Col 1:16-17: "by him were **all things created**, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible... **he is before all things**, and by him all things consist."
- Rev 4:11: "thou hast created all things, and **for thy pleasure they are and were created**."
- 1 Cor 8:6: "one God, the Father, **of whom are all things**."

## 3. Extra-Biblical Witness

- **2 Macc 7:28**: "look upon the heaven and the earth... and consider that **God made them of things that were not**." The earliest clear statement (2nd c. BC).
- **Shepherd of Hermas**: God "made out of what did not exist the things that exist".
- **Theophilus of Antioch** and **Irenaeus**: explicitly against Plato's pre-existing matter. If matter were uncreated, it would be equal to God. Irenaeus: "God... created matter itself."
- Gerhard May's thesis (*ex nihilo* was invented in the 2nd c. against Gnostics): Copan argues the 2nd c. made explicit what was already assumed. See [[The Forms and the Demiurge]].

## 4. What It Means

- Not that "nothing" is a stuff God used. It means God created **without any material cause**; everything other than God depends on Him for its existence.
- Includes a **temporal beginning** (the world isn't eternal) and **ongoing dependence** (conservation).
- Distinct from Aquinas, who held creation ex nihilo was compatible in principle with an eternal world (known by faith that it had a beginning). Craig argues for the beginning philosophically.

## 5. Abstract Objects

- If numbers, propositions, properties exist necessarily and independently of God (Platonism), then not "all things" were created. Problem for John 1:3.
- Options: God creates them; they're God's thoughts (divine conceptualism); they don't exist (nominalism). Craig later wrote *God Over All* on this. See [[The Problem of Universals: Realism vs Nominalism]].

## 6. Philosophical Arguments (Craig)

The **kalam** arguments against an infinite past:
1. **An actual infinite can't exist** (Hilbert's Hotel: absurdities when adding/removing guests). So an infinite series of past events can't exist.
2. **An actual infinite can't be formed by successive addition**: you can't count to infinity; so the past, formed one event after another, can't be infinite. Otherwise today could never arrive.

So the universe began to exist; whatever begins to exist has a cause; so the universe has a cause.

## 7. Scientific Evidence (Craig)

- **Big Bang cosmology**: the universe expanding from an initial singularity, about 13.8 billion years ago. Space and time themselves begin.
- **Borde–Guth–Vilenkin theorem** (2003): any universe that has on average been expanding has a past boundary, including inflationary and multiverse models.
- **Second law of thermodynamics**: if the universe were infinitely old, it would already be at heat death (equilibrium). It isn't.

## 8. Naturalistic Alternatives

| Model | Problem |
|---|---|
| steady state | refuted by the cosmic microwave background |
| oscillating universe | entropy builds up each cycle; no known mechanism for a bounce |
| vacuum fluctuation | the vacuum is not nothing; and it should produce universes everywhere |
| chaotic inflation | still needs a beginning (BGV) |
| quantum gravity (Hartle–Hawking) | uses imaginary time; the real-time interpretation still has a beginning |
