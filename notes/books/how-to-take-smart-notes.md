Ahrens' central claim: writing isn't the last step of research, it's the medium of thinking throughout. Notes are the unit of work.

## Three kinds of note

1. **Fleeting notes** — quick captures, thrown away within a day or two.
2. **Literature notes** — brief, in your own words, one source at a time. Most of this notebook's [[B-0001|book notes]] are this kind.
3. **Permanent notes** — one idea each, written to stand alone and linked to what's already there.

## Why links beat folders

A folder asks *where does this belong?* A link asks *what does this connect to?* The second question is the one that produces new ideas. This notebook uses both: sections are the folders, `[[links]]` and tags do the connecting. See [[How to use this notebook]].
